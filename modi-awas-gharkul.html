📋 About Modi Awas Gharkul Yojana

Modi Awas Gharkul Yojana is a housing initiative of the Government of Maharashtra aimed at supporting eligible households from the Other Backward Classes (OBC), Special Backward Classes (SBC) and other eligible groups covered by the scheme.

The scheme focuses on providing housing assistance to eligible families who do not have adequate permanent housing.

🎯 Main Objective

To support eligible families in constructing a permanent house and improving their housing conditions.

👨‍👩‍👧 Eligibility

  • Applicant should be a resident of Maharashtra, subject to applicable rules.
  • Applicant should belong to a beneficiary category covered by the scheme.
  • Applicant must satisfy the applicable income and housing conditions.
  • Priority and beneficiary selection are conducted according to government guidelines.
Important: Exact eligibility criteria, beneficiary categories and income limits should be checked according to the latest government guidelines.

💰 Key Benefits

  • Financial assistance towards construction of a permanent house for eligible beneficiaries.
  • Support for eligible families to improve their housing conditions.
  • Assistance is provided according to applicable government norms.
  • Housing support may be coordinated with other applicable housing programmes.

🏡 Scheme Features

🏠 Permanent Housing

The scheme focuses on supporting eligible families in obtaining permanent housing.

👨‍👩‍👧 Eligible Families

Eligible beneficiaries are selected according to the prescribed government criteria.

🌿 Maharashtra

The scheme is implemented in Maharashtra through concerned government authorities.

🏗️ Construction Assistance

Financial support is intended for eligible house construction according to scheme provisions.

📄 Documents / Information

  • Aadhaar card or approved identity proof.
  • Maharashtra residence proof, where applicable.
  • Caste/category certificate, where required.
  • Income certificate, where required.
  • Bank account details.
  • Land or house-related documents, where applicable.
  • Any additional documents requested by the concerned authority.

📝 How to Apply

  • Contact the concerned Gram Panchayat, local authority or government department.
  • Check the current eligibility and application process.
  • Obtain the prescribed application form or use the official online process, if available.
  • Submit the required documents.
  • Complete the verification process and track the application through the concerned authority.

⚠️ Important Information

Eligibility, beneficiary categories, financial assistance and application procedures may change according to Maharashtra Government guidelines.

Always verify the latest information before applying.
