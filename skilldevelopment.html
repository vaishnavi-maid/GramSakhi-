🛠 Skill Development

Overview

Skill Development programs help students, job seekers, and professionals improve their technical and professional skills. These programs provide industry-relevant training, certifications, and better employment opportunities.

Popular Skill Development Programs

Eligibility

  • Must be an Indian citizen.
  • Students, job seekers, and working professionals can apply.
  • Minimum qualification depends on the selected course.
  • Basic computer knowledge is beneficial for digital courses.

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Educational Certificates
  • Passport Size Photograph
  • Mobile Number
  • Email ID
  • Bank Account Details (if required)

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official Skill Development portal.
  2. Create your account.
  3. Choose a course based on your interests and qualifications.
  4. Upload the required documents.
  5. Complete the registration and start your training.
