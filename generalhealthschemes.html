About General Health Schemes

Government health schemes provide healthcare support, medical services and financial assistance to eligible citizens. GramSakhi brings important health scheme information together in one easy-to-use platform.

🏥 Ayushman Bharat - PM Jan Arogya Yojana

Purpose: Provides healthcare coverage to eligible families for specified hospital treatments.

Key Features:

  • Cashless treatment at empanelled hospitals
  • Coverage for eligible families
  • Hospitalization-related healthcare support

Eligibility: Based on the applicable government eligibility criteria.

🏥 National Health Mission (NHM)

Purpose: Strengthens public healthcare services and improves access to essential health services.

Key Areas:

  • Maternal and child healthcare
  • Primary healthcare services
  • Disease prevention and control
  • Community health services

Beneficiaries: Citizens accessing eligible government health services.

Know More

🏥 Mahatma Jyotirao Phule Jan Arogya Yojana

Purpose: A Maharashtra government health protection scheme providing healthcare assistance to eligible beneficiaries.

Key Features:

  • Cashless treatment at participating hospitals
  • Support for specified medical procedures
  • Healthcare assistance for eligible beneficiaries

State: Maharashtra

🩺 National Programme for Prevention and Control of Non-Communicable Diseases

Purpose: Supports prevention, screening and management of important non-communicable diseases.

Focus Areas:

  • Health screening
  • Disease awareness
  • Early detection
  • Access to appropriate healthcare services
