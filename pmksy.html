Scheme Name

Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchai Yojana (PMKSY)

Category

Irrigation & Water Management

Description

PMKSY promotes efficient irrigation, water conservation, and improved water use in agriculture through modern irrigation methods such as drip and sprinkler systems.

Eligibility

  • Indian farmers
  • Individual farmers and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs)
  • Eligible agricultural land owners

Benefits

  • Subsidy on drip irrigation
  • Subsidy on sprinkler irrigation
  • Water conservation support
  • Higher crop productivity
  • Reduced water wastage

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Land Records
  • Bank Passbook
  • Passport-size Photograph
  • Mobile Number

How to Apply

  1. Visit the nearest Agriculture Office.
  2. Fill out the PMKSY application form.
  3. Submit the required documents.
  4. Field verification will be conducted.
  5. Receive subsidy after approval.
