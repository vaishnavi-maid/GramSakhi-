📋 About the Campaign

The Accessible India Campaign, also known as Sugamya Bharat Abhiyan, is a Government of India initiative focused on improving accessibility for persons with disabilities.

It aims to make the built environment, transport systems and information and communication systems more accessible and inclusive.

🎯 Main Objective

To create an inclusive environment where persons with disabilities can access public places, transportation and information with greater ease and independence.

🏢 Major Areas of Accessibility

🏢 Built Environment

Improving accessibility in public buildings and other important infrastructure.

🚌 Transportation

Promoting accessibility in airports, railway stations and other transport facilities.

💻 Information & Communication

Promoting accessible digital content, websites and information systems.

📱 Digital Accessibility

Encouraging accessible technology and digital services for persons with disabilities.

🎁 How It Helps Persons with Disabilities

  • Better access to public buildings and facilities.
  • Improved accessibility in transportation.
  • Greater access to information and communication technologies.
  • Supports independent participation in education, employment and public life.
  • Promotes an inclusive and barrier-free environment.

🛠️ Examples of Accessibility Features

  • Ramps and accessible entrances.
  • Accessible toilets.
  • Lifts and other accessibility facilities.
  • Accessible transport facilities.
  • Signage and information designed for accessibility.
  • Accessible websites and digital services.

👥 Who Benefits?

The campaign primarily supports persons with disabilities, including people with different physical, sensory and other disabilities.

Improved accessibility can also benefit senior citizens, people with temporary disabilities and others who face barriers in accessing public facilities.

⚠️ Important Information

The Accessible India Campaign is primarily an accessibility and inclusion initiative rather than a direct financial assistance scheme.

Implementation of accessibility measures may vary between locations and institutions.
