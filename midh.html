Scheme Name

Mission for Integrated Development of Horticulture (MIDH)

Category

Horticulture Development

Description

MIDH promotes the holistic development of horticulture by supporting the cultivation of fruits, vegetables, spices, flowers, medicinal plants, and mushrooms. It also provides assistance for nurseries, protected cultivation, post-harvest management, and marketing.

Eligibility

  • Indian farmers
  • Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs)
  • Self Help Groups (SHGs)
  • Eligible horticulture growers

Benefits

  • Financial assistance for horticulture crops
  • Support for greenhouse and shade-net farming
  • Subsidy for nurseries and planting material
  • Post-harvest and storage assistance
  • Better productivity and income

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Land Records
  • Bank Passbook
  • Passport-size Photograph
  • Mobile Number

How to Apply

  1. Visit the District Horticulture Office.
  2. Fill out the application form.
  3. Submit the required documents.
  4. Field inspection will be conducted.
  5. Receive subsidy after approval.
