Scheme Name

Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF)

Category

Agricultural Infrastructure

Description

The Agriculture Infrastructure Fund provides medium- and long-term financial support for developing post-harvest management and community farming infrastructure such as warehouses, cold storage, grading units, and processing facilities.

Eligibility

  • Farmers
  • Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs)
  • Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS)
  • Self Help Groups (SHGs)
  • Agricultural entrepreneurs

Benefits

  • Interest subsidy on eligible loans
  • Credit guarantee support for eligible borrowers
  • Improved storage facilities
  • Reduced post-harvest losses
  • Better market access

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • PAN Card
  • Project Report
  • Bank Account Details
  • Identity & Address Proof

How to Apply

  1. Visit the Agriculture Infrastructure Fund portal or a participating bank.
  2. Register and complete the application.
  3. Upload the required documents.
  4. Submit your project proposal.
  5. Await approval and loan processing.
