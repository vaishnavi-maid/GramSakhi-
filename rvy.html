Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana

Scheme Overview

Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana (RVY) is a Government of India scheme that provides free assistive living devices to eligible senior citizens suffering from age-related disabilities.

Benefits

Eligibility

  • Applicant must be 60 years of age or above.
  • Must be an Indian citizen.
  • Should belong to the Below Poverty Line (BPL) category or other eligible category as per government rules.
  • Must be suffering from age-related disabilities such as poor vision, hearing loss or mobility problems.

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Age Proof
  • BPL Card/Income Certificate
  • Address Proof
  • Passport Size Photograph
  • Medical Certificate (if required)

How to Apply

  1. Visit the nearest Common Service Centre (CSC), Gram Panchayat, or district social welfare office.
  2. Fill out the Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana application form.
  3. Attach the required documents.
  4. Complete the verification process.
  5. Eligible beneficiaries will receive assistive devices through distribution camps.
