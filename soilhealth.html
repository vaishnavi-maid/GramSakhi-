Scheme Name

Soil Health Card Scheme

Category

Soil Testing & Agriculture

Description

The Soil Health Card Scheme helps farmers understand the nutrient status of their soil and recommends the correct fertilizers and nutrients for better crop production.

Eligibility

  • All Indian farmers
  • Farmers owning or cultivating agricultural land

Benefits

  • Free soil testing
  • Improves crop yield
  • Reduces fertilizer cost
  • Maintains soil fertility
  • Scientific farming guidance

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Land Records
  • Mobile Number
  • Passport-size Photograph

How to Apply

  1. Visit the nearest Agriculture Office or Soil Testing Laboratory.
  2. Submit a soil sample.
  3. Complete the application form.
  4. Wait for laboratory analysis.
  5. Collect your Soil Health Card.
