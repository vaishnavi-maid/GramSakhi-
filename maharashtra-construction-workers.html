📋 About Maharashtra Construction Workers Welfare

The Maharashtra Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board provides welfare and social security support to eligible registered construction workers in Maharashtra.

The Board provides benefits in areas such as education, social security, healthcare and financial assistance.

🎯 Main Objective

To improve the safety, health, education, financial security and overall welfare of eligible construction workers and their families.

✅ Who Can Register?

  • Worker should generally be between 18 and 60 years of age.
  • Worker should have worked for more than 90 days during the previous 12 months.
  • Worker should be engaged in eligible construction work.
  • Worker must complete registration with the Maharashtra Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.

📄 Documents for Registration

  • Proof of age.
  • 90 days work certificate.
  • Residence proof.
  • Identity proof.
  • Passport-size photographs.
Requirements can change according to the latest instructions of the Maharashtra Welfare Board.

🎁 Major Welfare Categories

Social Security

🛡️ Social Security Benefits

Includes selected social-security assistance such as marriage-related support and insurance-related schemes for eligible registered workers.

Education

🎓 Education Assistance

Educational assistance is available for eligible children of registered construction workers according to the applicable scheme conditions.

Health Care

🏥 Health Care Assistance

The Board provides selected healthcare assistance to eligible registered construction workers under its applicable schemes.

Financial Assistance

💰 Financial Assistance

Financial support is available for specified situations such as accidental death, natural death, funeral expenses and housing-related assistance.

⭐ Examples of Available Benefits

  • Education assistance for eligible children.
  • Marriage-related financial assistance under applicable schemes.
  • PMJJBY and PMSBY-related benefits for eligible registered workers.
  • Assistance in cases of specified disability.
  • Financial assistance in specified cases of accidental or natural death.
  • Funeral assistance.
  • Housing-related assistance under applicable schemes.

📝 How to Register

  • Visit the official Maharashtra BOCW Welfare Board website.
  • Select the worker registration option.
  • Enter your personal and work-related information.
  • Submit the required documents.
  • Complete the verification process.
  • Keep your registration details safely for applying for welfare benefits.

⚠️ Important Information

Only registered construction workers with active subscriptions can avail benefits from the Board, subject to the eligibility requirements of each individual scheme.

Benefit amounts, documents and eligibility conditions may differ from one scheme to another.
