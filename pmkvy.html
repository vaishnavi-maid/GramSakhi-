Scheme Name

Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY)

Category

Students / Youth

Description

PMKVY is a Government of India skill development scheme that provides free training and certification to improve employment opportunities.

Eligibility

  • Indian Citizen
  • Unemployed youth or students
  • Minimum educational qualification depends on the course

Benefits

  • Free skill training
  • Government certificate
  • Placement assistance
  • Industry-recognized skills

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Passport Photo
  • Educational Certificates
  • Mobile Number
  • Bank Account Details

How to Apply

  1. Visit the Skill India Portal.
  2. Register as a candidate.
  3. Select your preferred training course.
  4. Upload the required documents.
  5. Submit the application.
