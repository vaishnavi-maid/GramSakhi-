🚜 Why Farm Machinery is Important

Farm machinery helps farmers perform agricultural operations more efficiently. It can reduce manual labour, save time and improve the timeliness of farm operations.


Farmers should select machinery according to their farm size, crop, soil condition, available power, budget and actual requirement.

🚜 Common Farm Machinery

🚜 Tractor

Used for ploughing, tilling, sowing, transportation and operating different farm implements.

🌱 Rotavator

Used for preparing and pulverising soil before sowing.

🌾 Cultivator

Used for primary or secondary tillage depending on the implement and soil.

🌱 Seed Drill

Places seed in rows at controlled depth and spacing.

💧 Sprayer

Used to apply approved agricultural sprays uniformly to crops.

🌾 Thresher

Separates grains from harvested crop material.

🌿 Chaff Cutter

Cuts fodder into smaller pieces for livestock feeding.

🌾 Reaper

Helps in harvesting crops such as cereals and other suitable crops.

💰 Government Machinery Assistance & Schemes

Government programmes may provide assistance for agricultural machinery and equipment to eligible farmers or farmer groups. Availability, eligibility, subsidy percentage, approved machinery and limits can change according to the current guidelines.


🏛️ Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM)

The Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization aims to promote access to agricultural machinery and mechanization, including support through applicable government programmes.

🌐 Official Portal

🌾 Maharashtra Agriculture Department

🤝 Custom Hiring Centres

Custom Hiring Centres can help farmers access farm machinery on a hiring basis instead of purchasing every machine individually. Availability depends on the local programme and service provider.

📄 Common Documents

The exact documents depend on the scheme. Commonly requested documents may include:


  • 🪪 Aadhaar or other accepted identity document
  • 🏦 Bank account details
  • 🌾 Land or farmer-related records where applicable
  • 📱 Registered mobile number
  • 📄 Caste/category certificate where applicable
  • 📋 Other documents specified by the current scheme

💡 How to Choose the Right Machine

📏 Consider Farm Size

Choose machine capacity according to the size of your farm and expected workload.

🌾 Consider Your Crop

Different crops require different implements and harvesting equipment.

💰 Consider Cost

Compare purchase, fuel, maintenance and operating costs before buying.

🔧 Maintenance

Check availability of spare parts, servicing and repair facilities.

⚡ Power Requirement

Ensure that the tractor or power source is compatible with the implement.

🤝 Hiring Option

For occasional use, compare machine hiring or Custom Hiring Centre options with purchase.

⚠️ Farm Machinery Safety

  • Read the machine's operating instructions before use.
  • Keep children away from operating machinery.
  • Wear appropriate protective equipment.
  • Switch off the machine before cleaning or performing maintenance.
  • Do not remove safety guards while machinery is operating.
  • Use tractors and implements only within their recommended operating limits.
  • Ensure operators are properly trained.
  • Do not carry passengers in unsafe positions on tractors or agricultural machinery.
⚠️ Important:

Machinery subsidy percentages, maximum assistance, eligible farmers, approved machines, vendors and application dates can change. Always verify the latest information on the official government portal before purchasing machinery or applying for assistance.
