📋 About UDID

The Unique Disability ID (UDID) initiative aims to create a national database of persons with disabilities and provide a Unique Disability Identity Card to eligible persons.

The UDID system helps persons with disabilities access disability-related government services and benefits through a standardised identity document.

🎯 Main Objective

To create a comprehensive system for identification of persons with disabilities and make access to government benefits and services easier.

✅ Who Can Apply?

  • Persons with disabilities who meet the applicable eligibility requirements.
  • Applicants can apply through the official UDID system.
  • The disability is assessed and certified through the prescribed medical process.
  • The final disability percentage and certification are determined by the authorised medical authority.

⭐ Key Features

🪪 Unique Identity

Provides a unique disability identity number and card for eligible persons.

💻 Online System

The application and related services are available through the online UDID platform.

📄 Disability Certificate

The system supports digital processing and issuance of disability certificates through the prescribed process.

🏛️ Government Services

UDID can help beneficiaries access disability-related government schemes and services where required.

🎁 Benefits of UDID

  • Provides a recognised disability identity document.
  • Helps simplify access to disability-related government schemes and services.
  • Reduces the need to maintain multiple disability-related documents.
  • Helps maintain disability information through a centralised system.
  • Makes it easier to track the status of applications and certificates.

📄 Common Documents

  • Aadhaar card or another accepted identity document.
  • Address proof, where required.
  • Recent photograph.
  • Existing disability-related medical records or certificates, if available.
  • Other documents requested during the certification process.

📝 How to Apply for UDID

  1. Open the official UDID portal.
  2. Register or log in to your account.
  3. Fill in the required personal details.
  4. Upload the required documents.
  5. Select the appropriate medical authority/ hospital as available in the system.
  6. Attend the medical assessment when scheduled.
  7. After approval, obtain the disability certificate and UDID card through the prescribed process.

⚠️ Important Information

Disability certification is carried out by authorised medical authorities according to applicable rules.

Keep your registered mobile number and application details safe so that you can track your application.
