📋 About the Scheme

Ayushman Bharat - Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB PM-JAY) is a Government of India health assurance scheme that provides financial protection for eligible beneficiaries for specified hospitalisation services.

💰 Main Benefit

Eligible beneficiaries can receive health cover of up to ₹5 lakh per family per year for covered secondary and tertiary hospitalisation.

🎯 Objective

  • Reduce the financial burden of major healthcare expenses on eligible families.
  • Improve access to hospitalisation services.
  • Provide financial protection against covered medical expenses.
  • Improve access to quality healthcare services.

✅ Key Benefits

  • Health cover up to ₹5 lakh per family per year for eligible beneficiaries.
  • Cashless treatment at empanelled hospitals for covered services.
  • Coverage is provided on a family-floater basis under the applicable scheme rules.
  • Pre-existing conditions are covered from the beginning for eligible beneficiaries.
  • Eligible beneficiaries can access treatment through empanelled hospitals.

👨‍👩‍👧 Eligibility

Eligibility for the main PM-JAY beneficiary population is based on the government's notified beneficiary criteria and databases.

👨‍👩‍👧 Eligible Families

Families identified under the applicable government eligibility criteria can receive PM-JAY benefits.

👵 Senior Citizens 70+

Senior citizens aged 70 years and above are eligible for AB PM-JAY coverage irrespective of income, subject to applicable rules.

📄 Documents / Information

Documents and identification requirements may vary depending on the beneficiary verification process.

  • Aadhaar or other accepted identification
  • Mobile number
  • Family/beneficiary details
  • Other documents requested during verification

📝 How to Get the Benefit

  • Check whether you are an eligible beneficiary.
  • Complete beneficiary verification/e-KYC where required.
  • Obtain or use the applicable Ayushman card.
  • Visit an empanelled hospital for covered treatment.
  • Follow the hospital's beneficiary verification and treatment process.

⚠️ Important Information

Scheme eligibility, covered procedures, documents and application processes can change according to government rules.

Always verify the latest information through the official National Health Authority / PM-JAY portal before applying or seeking treatment.

🌐 Official Information

Use the official PM-JAY/National Health Authority resources for beneficiary verification, hospital information and the latest scheme details.
