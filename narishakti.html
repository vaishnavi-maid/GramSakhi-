Award Name

Nari Shakti Puraskar

Category

Women Empowerment Award

Description

Nari Shakti Puraskar is the highest civilian award for women in India. It recognizes individuals and organizations for exceptional work in women's empowerment and social development.

Eligibility

  • Indian women with outstanding achievements
  • Institutions and organizations working for women empowerment
  • Must meet government nomination criteria

Benefits

  • National recognition
  • Award certificate
  • Cash prize (as per government rules)
  • Recognition for contribution to society

Required Documents

  • Nomination Form
  • Identity Proof
  • Achievement Details
  • Supporting Documents
  • Passport-size Photograph

How to Apply

  1. Visit the Ministry of Women and Child Development website.
  2. Submit the nomination form.
  3. Upload the required documents.
  4. Wait for evaluation and selection.
