🌱 About GramSakhi

One Platform for Every Rural Citizen

🌱 What is GramSakhi?

GramSakhi is a Smart Digital Village Platform designed to bring important information and digital services closer to rural citizens. It provides useful information related to government schemes, students, farmers, healthcare, employment, emergency services, women empowerment and local businesses — all in one platform. The goal of GramSakhi is to reduce the information gap between rural citizens and available digital services.

"One Platform for Every Rural Citizen"

Connecting villages with information, opportunities and digital services.

🎯 Vision & Mission

🌍

Our Vision

To create digitally empowered villages where every citizen can easily access important information, government services and opportunities.

🚀

Our Mission

To provide a simple, accessible and user-friendly digital platform that helps rural citizens make better decisions and improve their quality of life.

💡 Why GramSakhi?

Many rural citizens face difficulties in finding correct information about government schemes, education, healthcare, employment and other services. GramSakhi brings this information together in one easy-to-use platform and aims to make digital information accessible to everyone.
