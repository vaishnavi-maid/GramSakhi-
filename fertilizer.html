🌱 Understanding NPK

🟢 Nitrogen (N)

Helps in leaf growth, plant development and green colour.

🟡 Phosphorus (P)

Supports root development, flowering, energy transfer and seed formation.

🔵 Potassium (K)

Supports overall plant strength, water regulation and crop quality.

🚜 How to Apply Fertilizer

  • Test your soil before applying fertilizers.
  • Use the fertilizer according to soil-test and crop recommendations.
  • Apply fertilizer at the recommended crop stage.
  • Keep fertilizer away from direct contact with seeds unless the method specifically allows it.
  • Irrigate appropriately after fertilizer application when recommended.
  • Avoid excessive fertilizer application.
  • Use protective equipment while handling fertilizers.
  • Store fertilizer in a dry and safe place.

🌾 General Crop-wise Nutrient Guidance

🥬 Vegetables

Vegetables require balanced nutrients. Nitrogen supports vegetative growth, while phosphorus and potassium support roots, flowering and quality.

🌾 Cereals

Crops such as wheat, maize, jowar and bajra require balanced nutrition. Nitrogen is especially important for healthy vegetative growth.

🌱 Pulses

Gram, tur, moong and urad have different nutrient requirements. Avoid unnecessary excess nitrogen and follow soil-test recommendations.

🍇 Fruit Crops

Fruit crops require balanced and stage-wise nutrition. Nutrient needs vary with crop, variety, plant age and production stage.

🌿 Oilseeds

Soybean and groundnut require balanced nutrition along with appropriate secondary and micronutrients when soil tests indicate a deficiency.

🌱 Cotton

Cotton requires balanced nutrition throughout its growth. Fertilizer application should follow soil-test and local recommendations.

🔬 Soil Testing

Soil testing helps identify nutrient levels and soil properties. It can help farmers choose appropriate fertilizers instead of applying fertilizer unnecessarily.

🌿 Organic Fertilizers

🌱 Farmyard Manure (FYM)

Well-decomposed farmyard manure can improve soil organic matter and soil structure.

🪱 Vermicompost

Vermicompost is prepared using earthworms and decomposed organic material. It can improve soil organic matter.

🍂 Compost

Compost is decomposed organic material that can be used as a source of organic matter and nutrients.

🌿 Green Manure

Green manure crops can be incorporated into soil to add organic matter and improve soil health.

⚠️ Important Safety Information

Do not decide fertilizer quantity only from general information on this page. The correct dose depends on the crop, variety, soil condition, previous crop, irrigation, growth stage and soil-test results. For exact fertilizer recommendations, consult the Agriculture Department, Krishi Vigyan Kendra or a qualified agriculture expert.
