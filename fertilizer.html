🌾 Understanding NPK

N — Nitrogen

Supports leafy growth and the formation of chlorophyll.

P — Phosphorus

Supports root development, energy transfer and early plant growth.

K — Potassium

Supports water regulation, plant strength and overall crop quality.

NPK labels show the percentage of nitrogen, phosphate and potash in a fertilizer. Select a grade and application rate only according to crop requirements and soil-test advice.

🪴 How to Apply Fertilizer Responsibly

  1. Test your soil and use the recommended nutrient plan.
  2. Read the product label and follow the recommended dose.
  3. Apply fertilizer at the correct crop growth stage.
  4. Avoid placing concentrated fertilizer directly against seeds or plant stems.
  5. Keep fertilizer away from wells, ponds, drains and waterways.
  6. Store fertilizer in its original labelled package, away from children, animals and food.
  7. Do not mix products unless the label or a qualified expert confirms compatibility.

🌱 Crop-wise Guidance

Different crops need different amounts and combinations of nutrients. There is no single fertilizer dose that is correct for every farm.

Cereals

Wheat, rice, maize and other cereals require balanced nutrient management based on soil testing and the crop stage.

Pulses

Gram, tur, moong and other pulses have specific nutrient needs. Follow local recommendations and use suitable inoculants where advised.

Vegetables

Vegetable nutrient needs vary by species and harvest stage. Avoid excessive nitrogen, which can affect growth and quality.

Fruits

Fruit trees may need nutrients at particular growth stages. Base fertilizer type and amount on soil, plant age and expert advice.

🍃 Organic Fertilizers

Compost

Made from decomposed organic materials; it can improve soil structure and add organic matter.

Vermicompost

Organic material processed by earthworms. Quality and nutrient content depend on the source and preparation.

Farmyard Manure

Well-decomposed manure can add organic matter and nutrients. Use safe, mature material.

Neem Cake

A neem-seed by-product sometimes used as an organic soil amendment. It is not a replacement for every crop nutrient requirement.

🧫 Soil Testing

Soil testing helps identify nutrient status and supports more appropriate fertilizer decisions. Contact your local agriculture office, soil-testing laboratory or Krishi Vigyan Kendra for guidance on collecting a representative soil sample.
