📋 About PMJJBY

Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY) is a government-backed life insurance scheme available to eligible subscribers.

It provides life insurance protection to the insured person during the policy period, subject to the applicable insurance terms and conditions.

🎯 Main Objective

To provide affordable life insurance protection to eligible individuals and provide financial support to the nominee in case of covered death.

✅ Who Can Join?

  • Individuals generally between 18 and 50 years of age can enrol, subject to applicable scheme rules.
  • The subscriber should have an eligible bank or post-office account.
  • The account should support the required premium auto-debit facility.
  • The subscriber must provide consent for the annual premium deduction.
Important: New enrolment after the prescribed entry age is generally not permitted. Existing coverage may continue according to the scheme's renewal conditions.

💰 Annual Premium

The current annual premium for PMJJBY is:

₹436 per year

The premium is generally collected through auto-debit from the linked bank or post-office account.

🎁 Insurance Benefit

❤️ Life Cover

A life insurance benefit of ₹2 lakh is payable to the nominee in case of death of the insured member during the covered period, subject to policy terms.

💰 Affordable Premium

The scheme provides life insurance protection at a relatively low annual premium.

🛡️ What Does PMJJBY Cover?

PMJJBY provides life insurance cover for death of the insured member during the period of insurance.

💡 Key Point

Unlike PMSBY, which provides accident insurance, PMJJBY is a life insurance scheme.

📄 Required Information

  • Bank or post-office account.
  • Aadhaar or other required identification details as requested.
  • Mobile number, where required.
  • Nominee details.
  • Consent for premium auto-debit.

📝 How to Enrol

  • Visit your participating bank or post-office.
  • Ask for PMJJBY enrolment or consent form.
  • Provide the required personal and nominee information.
  • Give consent for annual premium auto-debit.
  • Submit the required form.

📑 How to Claim

  • The nominee should contact the concerned bank/post-office or insurer after the insured member's death.
  • Obtain and complete the prescribed claim form.
  • Submit the required supporting documents.
  • The insurer processes the claim according to the policy conditions.
Claim documentation and procedure may vary. The nominee should follow the latest instructions of the participating bank, post-office or insurer.

⚠️ Important Information

PMJJBY is a life insurance scheme. It does not provide general medical or health insurance.

Premium, eligibility, renewal, exclusions and claim conditions are subject to the latest official scheme and insurance policy terms.
