Scheme Name

Mahila e-Haat

Category

Women Entrepreneurship

Description

Mahila e-Haat is an online marketing platform launched by the Government of India to help women entrepreneurs and Self Help Groups (SHGs) sell their products and services online.

Eligibility

  • Indian women entrepreneurs
  • Women Self Help Groups (SHGs)
  • Women-owned businesses

Benefits

  • Free online marketplace
  • Direct connection with buyers
  • Promotes women entrepreneurs
  • Increases business opportunities
  • Digital marketing support

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Identity Proof
  • Bank Account Details
  • Business Details
  • Passport-size Photograph

How to Apply

  1. Visit the Mahila e-Haat portal.
  2. Create your account.
  3. Complete your business profile.
  4. Upload product details and photographs.
  5. Publish your products for sale.
