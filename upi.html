📋 About the Programme

The Universal Immunization Programme (UIP) is a Government of India programme that provides vaccination services through the public health system.

🎯 Main Purpose

To protect children and pregnant women against vaccine-preventable diseases through routine immunization services.

🎯 Objectives

  • Protect children against vaccine-preventable diseases.
  • Provide routine immunization through government health facilities.
  • Protect pregnant women against selected vaccine-preventable diseases.
  • Improve vaccination coverage across communities.
  • Reduce illness and deaths caused by vaccine-preventable diseases.

👶 Who Can Receive Vaccination?

👶 Children

Children receive vaccines according to the National Immunization Schedule and applicable government guidelines.

🤰 Pregnant Women

Pregnant women receive recommended vaccinations according to the national immunization schedule.

💉 Vaccination Services

🍼 Birth Vaccines

Vaccines recommended at birth are provided according to the National Immunization Schedule.

👶 Infant Vaccines

Infants receive vaccines at specified ages according to the national schedule.

🧒 Childhood Vaccines

Additional vaccines and doses are provided during childhood as recommended.

🤰 Maternal Vaccination

Recommended vaccination is provided to pregnant women through government health services.

📝 How to Get Vaccination

  • Visit your nearest government health facility.
  • Ask the health worker about the child's or mother's vaccination schedule.
  • Carry the vaccination/immunization record if available.
  • Follow the recommended vaccination schedule.
  • Ask the healthcare worker about any missed doses.

⚠️ Important Information

Vaccines and vaccination schedules should be followed according to the current National Immunization Schedule and advice from qualified healthcare professionals.

If a vaccination dose has been missed, contact your nearest government health facility for guidance on the appropriate schedule.
