📋 About Shabari Awas Yojana

Shabari Awas Yojana is a housing programme of the Government of Maharashtra intended to provide housing assistance to eligible Scheduled Tribe (ST) beneficiaries.

🎯 Main Objective

To support eligible tribal families in obtaining a permanent house and improving their housing conditions.

👨‍👩‍👧 Eligibility

  • Applicant should be a resident of Maharashtra, subject to applicable rules.
  • Applicant should belong to the Scheduled Tribe category covered by the scheme.
  • Applicant must satisfy the applicable economic and housing conditions.
  • The applicant should meet the land or house related requirements, where applicable.
Important: Eligibility conditions and beneficiary selection are subject to current Maharashtra Government guidelines.

💰 Key Benefits

  • Financial assistance for construction of a permanent house for eligible beneficiaries.
  • Support to improve housing conditions of eligible tribal families.
  • Assistance is provided according to the applicable government norms.

🏠 Scheme Features

🌿 Tribal Housing

The scheme focuses on housing support for eligible Scheduled Tribe beneficiaries.

🏠 Permanent House

Assistance is intended to help eligible families construct a permanent home.

💰 Financial Assistance

Eligible beneficiaries may receive financial support according to scheme provisions.

🌿 Maharashtra

The scheme is implemented in Maharashtra through the concerned government authorities.

📄 Documents / Information

  • Aadhaar card or other approved identity proof.
  • Maharashtra residence proof, where applicable.
  • Scheduled Tribe certificate.
  • Income certificate, where required.
  • Bank account details.
  • Land or housing documents, where applicable.
  • Other documents requested by the concerned authority.

📝 How to Apply

  • Contact the concerned Tribal Development Department or local government authority.
  • Check whether applications are currently being accepted.
  • Obtain the prescribed application form or use the official online process, if available.
  • Submit the required documents.
  • Track the application through the concerned authority.

⚠️ Important Information

The eligibility criteria, assistance amount, documents and application procedure may change according to Maharashtra Government rules.

Always verify the latest information with the concerned department before applying.
