📋 About PMSBY

Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) is a government-backed accident insurance scheme for eligible individuals.

The scheme provides financial protection in case of death or disability resulting from an accident, subject to the terms and conditions of the insurance cover.

🎯 Main Objective

To provide affordable accident insurance protection to eligible people at a low annual premium.

✅ Who Can Join?

  • Individuals generally in the age group of 18 to 70 years can enrol, subject to applicable scheme rules.
  • The applicant should have an eligible bank or post-office account.
  • The account should be enabled for the required premium auto-debit facility.
  • The subscriber must provide consent for the annual premium to be debited.
Note: Eligibility and coverage are subject to the latest PMSBY rules and insurance terms.

💰 Premium

The annual premium for PMSBY is:

₹20 per year

The premium is generally collected through auto-debit from the linked bank or post-office account.

🎁 Insurance Benefits

⚫ Accidental Death

Up to ₹2 lakh may be payable to the nominee in case of accidental death, subject to policy conditions.

♿ Total Disability

Up to ₹2 lakh may be payable for specified total permanent disabilities, subject to policy conditions.

🦽 Partial Disability

Up to ₹1 lakh may be payable for specified partial permanent disabilities, subject to policy conditions.

🛡️ Financial Protection

Provides financial support to the insured or nominee for covered accidental events.

⚠️ Covered Accidents

PMSBY provides cover for specified accidental death and disabilities.

Examples may include accidents resulting in specified permanent disabilities, subject to the policy terms.

The exact circumstances covered and exclusions are determined by the official insurance policy. Always check the current policy documents.

📄 Required Information

  • Bank or post-office account.
  • Aadhaar or other required identification details as requested by the participating institution.
  • Mobile number, where required.
  • Nominee details.
  • Consent for premium auto-debit.

📝 How to Enrol

  • Visit your participating bank or post-office.
  • Ask for PMSBY enrolment or consent form.
  • Provide the required information.
  • Give consent for annual premium auto-debit.
  • Submit the form to the concerned institution.

📑 How to Make a Claim

  • Inform the bank or post office and concerned insurer about the accident.
  • Obtain and complete the required claim form.
  • Submit the required supporting documents.
  • The claim is examined according to the applicable insurance terms.
Claim requirements can vary according to the circumstances of the accident. Contact the participating bank/post office or insurer for the latest procedure.

⚠️ Important Information

PMSBY is accident insurance. It is not a general health-insurance scheme.

Coverage, exclusions, enrolment period, premium and claim conditions are governed by the latest official PMSBY policy terms.

🌐 Official Information

For the latest information about PMSBY, eligibility and enrolment, check official government and participating-bank information.
