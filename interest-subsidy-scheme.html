📋 About the Scheme

The Interest Subsidy Scheme (ISS) is a vertical under Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Urban 2.0 (PMAY-U 2.0).

It provides interest subsidy support on eligible housing loans to help reduce the financial burden of eligible beneficiaries purchasing or constructing a house.

🎯 Main Objective

To make home loans more affordable for eligible urban families by providing government support towards interest on eligible housing loans.

👨‍👩‍👧 Eligibility

  • Applicant should satisfy the eligibility conditions prescribed under PMAY-U 2.0.
  • Applicant should belong to an eligible income category.
  • Applicant must have an eligible housing loan from an applicable lending institution.
  • The house and loan must satisfy the applicable scheme conditions.
Important: Eligibility, income limits, loan limits and other conditions should be checked according to the latest PMAY-U 2.0 guidelines before applying.

💰 Key Benefits

  • Government interest subsidy on eligible housing loans.
  • Helps reduce the effective cost of a housing loan.
  • Can reduce the financial burden of monthly housing-loan repayments.
  • Supports eligible families in purchasing or constructing a house.

👥 Beneficiary Categories

🏠 EWS

Economically Weaker Section beneficiaries who satisfy the applicable scheme requirements.

👨‍👩‍👧 LIG

Low Income Group beneficiaries who meet the prescribed conditions.

🏢 MIG

Eligible Middle Income Group beneficiaries covered under the applicable ISS provisions.

🏦 Home Loan Applicants

Applicants must obtain an eligible housing loan through an applicable lending institution.

🔄 How the Interest Subsidy Works

  • The beneficiary obtains an eligible housing loan from an approved lending institution.
  • The loan application is checked against the applicable PMAY-U 2.0 ISS conditions.
  • The eligible subsidy is processed through the prescribed system.
  • The subsidy reduces the effective burden of the eligible housing loan.

📄 Common Documents / Information

  • Aadhaar card or accepted identity proof.
  • Income-related documents.
  • Address/residence proof, where required.
  • Bank account details.
  • Housing loan documents.
  • Property or house-related documents, where applicable.
  • Any additional documents requested by the lending institution or authority.

📝 How to Apply

  • Check whether you satisfy the current PMAY-U 2.0 ISS eligibility conditions.
  • Approach an eligible lending institution offering the scheme.
  • Submit the required housing-loan and beneficiary documents.
  • Ask the lending institution to process the eligible PMAY-U 2.0 interest subsidy.
  • Track the application and subsidy status through the prescribed process.

⚠️ Important Information

Interest subsidy is available only when the applicant and housing loan satisfy the applicable PMAY-U 2.0 Interest Subsidy Scheme conditions.

The subsidy amount, income limits, loan conditions and other requirements may change according to government guidelines.

Always verify the latest information before applying.
