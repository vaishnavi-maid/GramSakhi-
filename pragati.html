Scheme Name

AICTE Pragati Scholarship

Category

Girl Students

Description

The AICTE Pragati Scholarship provides financial assistance to meritorious girl students pursuing technical education.

Eligibility

  • Indian Citizen
  • Girl student admitted to an AICTE-approved institution
  • Family income should be within the scheme limit

Benefits

  • Scholarship amount for tuition and educational expenses
  • Support for technical education

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Admission Letter
  • Income Certificate
  • Bank Passbook
  • Passport-size Photo

How to Apply

  1. Visit the AICTE Scholarship Portal.
  2. Register and log in.
  3. Fill out the application form.
  4. Upload the required documents.
  5. Submit the application.
