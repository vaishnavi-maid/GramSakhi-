📋 About Ayushman Arogya Mandir

Ayushman Arogya Mandirs are government primary healthcare facilities designed to provide comprehensive primary healthcare services closer to communities.

🎯 Main Purpose

To make essential primary healthcare, prevention, health promotion and basic treatment services more accessible to people in their communities.

🎯 Objectives

  • Provide accessible primary healthcare services.
  • Promote prevention and early detection of diseases.
  • Provide health promotion and wellness services.
  • Improve access to essential medicines and diagnostic services.
  • Connect patients with higher-level healthcare facilities when referral is required.

🩺 Services Available

🩺 General Healthcare

Basic consultation, treatment and management of common health conditions.

🤰 Maternal Healthcare

Primary healthcare and support related to pregnancy and maternal health.

👶 Child Healthcare

Basic child health services, monitoring and referral where required.

💉 Immunization

Vaccination and immunization services as provided through the public health system.

🩸 Disease Screening

Screening and early detection of selected diseases and health conditions.

💊 Medicines

Access to essential medicines according to services and availability at the facility.

🧪 Diagnostics

Selected diagnostic services according to facility capabilities.

🧘 Wellness

Health promotion, wellness and preventive healthcare activities.

👨‍👩‍👧 Who Can Use These Services?

Ayushman Arogya Mandirs are intended to provide primary healthcare services to people in the communities they serve.

  • Women
  • Children
  • Adults
  • Senior citizens
  • Adolescents
  • People requiring primary healthcare

📝 How to Access

  • Find the nearest Ayushman Arogya Mandir or government primary healthcare facility.
  • Visit the facility during its working hours.
  • Carry an identity document if required.
  • Consult the healthcare worker or medical staff.
  • Follow referral advice if specialist or higher-level treatment is required.

⚠️ Important Information

Services can vary depending on the facility, location, staffing and availability.

For the latest information about a particular facility, contact the nearest government health centre or check the official health department resources.
