📋 About NAP-SDP

The National Action Plan for Skill Development of Persons with Disabilities (NAP-SDP) focuses on improving skill development and employment opportunities for persons with disabilities.

The programme supports skill training through suitable training institutions and encourages persons with disabilities to develop skills relevant to employment and livelihoods.

🎯 Main Objective

To enhance the skills, employability and economic independence of persons with disabilities through suitable skill development and training opportunities.

✅ Who Can Benefit?

  • Eligible persons with disabilities who want to develop employment-related skills.
  • Candidates meeting the requirements of the selected training programme.
  • Beneficiaries may receive training through approved or participating training institutions.

🛠️ Skill Development Areas

💻 Digital Skills

Training may include computer and digital skills suitable for employment.

🔧 Technical Skills

Candidates may receive training in suitable technical and vocational areas.

🏢 Employment Skills

Training can help candidates develop workplace and job-related skills.

💼 Entrepreneurship

Skill development can support self-employment and livelihood opportunities.

🎁 Key Benefits

  • Access to skill development opportunities.
  • Training designed to improve employability.
  • Development of job-related technical and soft skills.
  • Better opportunities for employment and livelihood.
  • Support towards greater economic independence.

📌 General Eligibility

  • Applicant should be a person with disability satisfying the applicable criteria.
  • Applicant must meet the age and educational requirements of the selected training programme.
  • A valid disability certificate or UDID may be required.
  • Additional requirements may vary according to the training programme.

📄 Common Documents

  • Disability certificate / UDID.
  • Aadhaar or accepted identity proof.
  • Educational certificates, where required.
  • Address proof.
  • Passport-size photograph.
  • Bank details, where required.

📝 How to Get Skill Training

  1. Find an approved or participating skill training centre.
  2. Check the available courses and eligibility.
  3. Register for the suitable training programme.
  4. Submit the required documents.
  5. Complete the prescribed training.
  6. Use the acquired skills for employment, self-employment or further opportunities.

⚠️ Important Information

Training courses, eligibility, age limits and other conditions may vary according to the programme and implementing agency.

Applicants should check the latest official information before registering.
