Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme

Scheme Overview

The Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS) provides monthly financial assistance to elderly citizens belonging to Below Poverty Line (BPL) families.

Benefits

Eligibility

  • Applicant must be 60 years or above.
  • Must belong to a Below Poverty Line (BPL) family.
  • Should be an Indian citizen.
  • Should not be receiving another government pension (where applicable).

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Age Proof
  • Income Certificate
  • BPL Ration Card (if applicable)
  • Bank Passbook
  • Passport Size Photograph

How to Apply

  1. Visit your Gram Panchayat, Municipal Office, or the official pension portal.
  2. Fill out the application form.
  3. Attach the required documents.
  4. Submit the application for verification.
  5. After approval, the pension amount will be credited to your bank account.
