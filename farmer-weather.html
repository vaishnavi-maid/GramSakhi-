🌾 Weather-Based Farming Tips

  • Check rainfall forecasts before irrigation.
  • Avoid unnecessary irrigation when rain is expected.
  • Check wind conditions before spraying pesticides.
  • Protect harvested crops during expected rainfall.
  • Take precautions during extreme heat or heavy rain.
  • Plan sowing and harvesting according to suitable weather conditions.
