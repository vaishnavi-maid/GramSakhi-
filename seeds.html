🌱 Seed Information

Choose Quality Seeds for a Healthy Crop

🌱 Why Good Seeds Matter

Good-quality seed is an important starting point for a healthy crop. Seed quality can affect germination, plant establishment, crop uniformity and ultimately crop performance.


Farmers should select seed according to the crop, local growing conditions, recommended variety and the purpose for which the crop is being grown.

🔍 What is Quality Seed?

🌱 Good Germination

Quality seed should have good germination according to the applicable seed standard and product information.

🧬 Varietal Purity

The seed should correspond to the variety mentioned on its official label.

🦠 Seed Health

Seed should be free from serious pest, disease and contamination problems as far as applicable to the crop and seed class.

💧 Appropriate Moisture

Seed should be stored under conditions that help maintain its quality and viability.

🌾 Common Types of Seed

🏷️ Certified Seed

Seed produced and processed under applicable certification standards and carrying the required certification information.

🌱 Hybrid Seed

Hybrid seed is produced by crossing selected parent lines to obtain desired characteristics. Hybrid seed generally needs to be purchased again for each crop cycle.

🌾 Open-Pollinated Varieties

These varieties can produce seed that may be saved under appropriate conditions, but farmers should follow crop-specific recommendations.

🌿 Improved Varieties

Improved varieties are developed or selected for desirable characteristics such as yield, quality, maturity or adaptation.

🛒 How to Select Seeds Before Buying

1

🏷️ Check the Label

Check crop name, variety, lot or batch details, germination information, validity and other required information on the seed package.

2

📅 Check the Date

Check the relevant packing, testing and validity information provided on the package.

3

🏪 Buy From a Reliable Source

Purchase seed from authorised or reliable agricultural input dealers and keep the bill.

4

🌱 Check Seed Quality

Avoid packages that appear damaged, wet, open or improperly stored.

5

📋 Choose the Right Variety

Select a variety suitable for your crop, season, soil, climate and local conditions.

6

🧾 Keep the Bill

Keep the purchase bill, package label and other documents safely for future reference.

🧪 Seed Treatment

Seed treatment can help protect seeds and young seedlings from certain seed-borne or soil-borne problems when an appropriate treatment is recommended for the crop.


🌱 Biological Treatment

Certain beneficial microorganisms can be used for seed treatment where recommended for the specific crop.

🧪 Chemical Treatment

Use only a registered product specifically recommended for the crop and purpose. Follow the product label exactly.

🦠 Disease Protection

Appropriate seed treatment may help reduce certain seed-borne disease risks.

⚠️ Expert Advice

Treatment requirements differ by crop and seed. Follow agricultural expert or official recommendations.

📦 Proper Seed Storage

🌬️ Keep Dry

Store seed in a dry environment and protect it from moisture.

🌡️ Avoid Excess Heat

Protect seed from excessive heat and direct sunlight.

🐀 Protect From Pests

Keep stored seed protected from rodents, insects and other pests.

🏷️ Keep Labels

Store seed in its original labelled package where possible and keep the purchase details.

🌱 Important Points Before Sowing

  • Select the appropriate crop and variety for the local season and conditions.
  • Prepare the field according to the crop's requirements.
  • Use the recommended seed rate for the specific crop and variety.
  • Follow recommended sowing depth and spacing.
  • Ensure adequate soil moisture at sowing.
  • Avoid sowing damaged or poor-quality seed.
  • Follow local Agriculture Department or agricultural university recommendations for specific crops.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid


  • Buying seed only because it is cheap.
  • Buying packages with damaged or unclear labels.
  • Ignoring the recommended variety for the local season.
  • Using an incorrect seed rate.
  • Storing seed in a damp place.
  • Using old or improperly stored seed.
  • Using seed treatment chemicals without checking the label or recommendation.
🚫 Important:

Do not use an unlabelled or illegally marketed seed packet. Do not assume that the same seed rate, spacing or treatment is suitable for every variety. Always check the current information on the seed package and follow recommendations from the Agriculture Department, agricultural university or qualified agricultural expert.
