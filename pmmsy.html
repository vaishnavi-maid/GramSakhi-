Scheme Name

Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY)

Category

Fisheries & Aquaculture

Description

PMMSY promotes sustainable fisheries, fish farming, aquaculture infrastructure, cold chain facilities, and income generation for fish farmers across India.

Eligibility

  • Fish farmers
  • Fishermen
  • Fish Farmer Producer Organizations (FFPOs)
  • Entrepreneurs in fisheries and aquaculture

Benefits

  • Financial assistance for fish farming
  • Support for pond development
  • Cold storage and transportation assistance
  • Employment generation
  • Higher fish production and income

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Identity Proof
  • Bank Passbook
  • Project Report (if applicable)
  • Passport-size Photograph

How to Apply

  1. Visit the nearest Fisheries Department Office.
  2. Fill out the PMMSY application form.
  3. Submit the required documents.
  4. Verification by the Fisheries Department.
  5. Receive assistance after approval.
