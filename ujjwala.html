Scheme Name

Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)

Category

Women

Description

PM Ujjwala Yojana provides free LPG gas connections to eligible women from poor households to promote clean cooking fuel.

Eligibility

  • Indian Citizen
  • Adult woman (18 years or above)
  • Eligible household as per government guidelines

Benefits

  • Free LPG connection
  • Cleaner cooking fuel
  • Improved health
  • Reduced indoor air pollution

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Ration Card
  • Address Proof
  • Bank Account Details
  • Passport-size Photo

How to Apply

  1. Visit the nearest LPG distributor.
  2. Fill out the PMUY application form.
  3. Submit the required documents.
  4. Wait for verification.
  5. Receive the LPG connection after approval.
