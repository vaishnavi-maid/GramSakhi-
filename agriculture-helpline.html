🔎 What Kind of Help Do You Need?

🌱 Crop Problem

For pest, disease, crop growth or other crop-related problems, contact the Agriculture Department or a qualified agricultural expert.

🧪 Soil Testing

For soil testing and soil health information, contact the appropriate local agriculture or soil testing service.

💧 Irrigation

For irrigation-related guidance, consult the Agriculture Department or relevant irrigation service.

🚜 Farm Machinery

For machinery assistance and current subsidy information, check the MahaDBT Farmer Portal and Agriculture Department.

💰 Government Schemes

Check the official MahaDBT Farmer Portal for eligible schemes, applications and status.

🌾 Agriculture Information

For official agricultural information, visit the Maharashtra Agriculture Department website.

1

📝 Identify Your Problem

Clearly identify whether your issue is related to crop, scheme, machinery, soil, irrigation or another agricultural service.

2

📷 Keep Information Ready

For crop problems, keep details such as crop name, variety, symptoms and photographs if available.

3

📞 Contact Official Support

Use the official contact number or website provided on this page.

4

📄 Keep Documents Ready

For scheme-related questions, keep your application details and relevant documents available.

📄 Before Calling About a Scheme

  • Keep your Farmer ID available if you have one.
  • Keep your application number available.
  • Keep your registered mobile number available.
  • Keep relevant land or scheme documents available when required.
  • Take a screenshot of any portal error message.
  • Never share your OTP or password with anyone.

🚨 Important Safety Information

Agriculture helplines are for agricultural information and support. For immediate threats to life, fire, serious accidents or other emergencies, use the appropriate emergency service rather than waiting for an agriculture department response.

⚠️ Beware of Fraud


  • Do not share your OTP with anyone.
  • Do not share your ATM PIN, UPI PIN or password.
  • Do not pay money to unknown persons promising a government subsidy.
  • Verify schemes and application information only through official government portals.
  • Keep your application receipt and documents safe.
