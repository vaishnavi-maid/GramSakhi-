📋 About the Scheme

Atal Bandhkam Kamgar Awas Yojana is a housing assistance initiative associated with the Maharashtra Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare framework.

The scheme is intended to support eligible registered construction workers in improving their housing conditions.

🎯 Main Objective

To provide housing-related financial assistance to eligible registered construction workers according to applicable government rules.

👷 Eligibility

  • Applicant should be a registered construction worker under the applicable Maharashtra construction workers welfare system.
  • Applicant must satisfy the prescribed registration and eligibility conditions.
  • Applicant should meet the applicable housing and other scheme requirements.
  • Beneficiary selection and assistance are subject to the current rules of the scheme.
Important: Registration as a construction worker alone does not automatically guarantee every benefit. Eligibility must be verified under the current scheme rules.

💰 Key Benefits

  • Housing-related financial assistance for eligible construction workers.
  • Support for improving or obtaining suitable housing according to applicable provisions.
  • Assistance is provided subject to approval and prescribed conditions.

🏠 Scheme Features

👷 Construction Workers

The scheme focuses on eligible registered construction workers.

🏠 Housing Support

Provides housing-related assistance according to scheme provisions.

🌿 Maharashtra

Implemented under the Maharashtra construction workers welfare framework.

📋 Registration

Beneficiaries need to satisfy applicable registration and scheme conditions.

📄 Common Documents / Information

  • Construction worker registration details.
  • Aadhaar card or approved identity document.
  • Bank account details.
  • Address/residence proof, where required.
  • Housing or property-related documents, where applicable.
  • Any additional documents requested by the concerned authority.

📝 How to Apply

  • Confirm that your construction-worker registration is valid.
  • Check the current eligibility requirements for the housing benefit.
  • Apply through the prescribed Maharashtra construction workers welfare process.
  • Submit the required documents.
  • Track the application through the concerned authority.

⚠️ Important Information

Scheme eligibility, assistance amount, required documents and application procedure may change according to current Maharashtra Government guidelines.

Verify the latest information before applying.

For current information about construction worker welfare schemes, registration and benefits, visit the official Maharashtra Government portal.
