Scheme Name

Skill India Mission

Category

Students / Youth

Description

Skill India Mission is a Government of India initiative to provide skill training, vocational education and employment opportunities to youth.

Eligibility

  • Indian Citizen
  • Students or unemployed youth
  • Eligible as per selected training program

Benefits

  • Free skill training
  • Government certificate
  • Placement support
  • Industry-ready skills

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Passport Photo
  • Educational Certificates
  • Mobile Number
  • Bank Account Details

How to Apply

  1. Visit the Skill India Portal.
  2. Register yourself.
  3. Select your training course.
  4. Upload the required documents.
  5. Submit the application.
