National Scholarship Portal

Scheme Name

National Scholarship Portal (NSP)

Category

Students

Eligibility

Students studying in schools or colleges who satisfy the eligibility of the selected scholarship.

Benefits

Financial assistance for education through Central and State Government scholarships.

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Income Certificate
  • Caste Certificate (if applicable)
  • Previous Marksheet
  • Bank Passbook
  • Passport Size Photo

How to Apply

1. Visit the National Scholarship Portal.
2. Register yourself.
3. Fill the application form.
4. Upload required documents.
5. Submit the application.

Description

National Scholarship Portal is the Government of India's single platform where students can apply for different scholarships.
