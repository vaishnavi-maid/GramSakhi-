💡 General Farming Tips

  • Test your soil before selecting fertilizer.
  • Use good-quality and certified seeds.
  • Follow recommended spacing while planting.
  • Avoid unnecessary irrigation.
  • Regularly check crops for pests and diseases.
  • Use fertilizers according to soil-test recommendations.
  • Harvest crops at the proper maturity stage.
  • Store harvested produce in a clean and dry place.
⚠️ Important: Crop requirements vary according to soil, weather, variety and local conditions. For fertilizer and pesticide recommendations, farmers should follow the advice of the Agriculture Department or a qualified agriculture expert.
