📋 About National Health Mission

The National Health Mission (NHM) is a major government health programme aimed at strengthening public healthcare services and improving access to affordable and quality healthcare.

🎯 Main Purpose

To improve the availability, accessibility and quality of healthcare services, particularly for vulnerable and underserved populations.

🎯 Objectives

  • Improve access to essential healthcare services.
  • Strengthen public health infrastructure.
  • Improve maternal and child health.
  • Support prevention and control of diseases.
  • Improve healthcare services in rural and underserved areas.
  • Promote affordable and quality healthcare.

🏥 Major Areas Covered

🤰 Maternal Health

Supports programmes and services for pregnant women and mothers.

👶 Child Health

Supports newborn and child healthcare, screening and treatment services.

💉 Immunization

Supports vaccination and immunization services through public health facilities.

🦠 Disease Control

Supports prevention, detection and control of communicable and non-communicable diseases.

🏘️ Rural Healthcare

Strengthens healthcare delivery in rural and underserved communities.

👩‍⚕️ Community Health

Supports community-level healthcare and health awareness activities.

🩺 Healthcare Services

  • Primary healthcare services
  • Maternal and reproductive healthcare
  • Newborn and child healthcare
  • Adolescent healthcare
  • Immunization services
  • Health screening and disease prevention
  • Health awareness and community programmes
  • Referral and treatment services

👨‍👩‍👧 Who Benefits?

NHM supports public healthcare services for communities across India, with particular attention to vulnerable and underserved populations.

  • Women
  • Children
  • Adolescents
  • Rural communities
  • Low-income and vulnerable populations
  • People requiring public health services

📝 How to Access NHM Services

  • Visit your nearest government health facility.
  • Contact an ASHA worker or other frontline health worker where applicable.
  • Visit a Sub-Centre, Primary Health Centre, Community Health Centre or government hospital.
  • Ask the healthcare staff about available government health services.

⚠️ Important Information

NHM is a broad government health mission that supports many different health programmes and services. Specific services, eligibility conditions and facilities can vary by programme and state.

Always check with the nearest government health facility for the latest information and available services.
