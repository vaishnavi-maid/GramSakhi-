Scheme Name

Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

Category

Agricultural Loan Scheme

Description

Kisan Credit Card provides farmers with easy and affordable credit for farming, crop production, livestock, fisheries, and other agricultural needs.

Eligibility

  • Farmers owning or cultivating agricultural land
  • Tenant farmers and sharecroppers
  • Self Help Groups (SHGs) and Joint Liability Groups (JLGs)

Benefits

  • Low-interest agricultural loans
  • Easy repayment options
  • Credit for crop production and allied activities
  • Insurance benefits on eligible cards

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • PAN Card (if required)
  • Land Records
  • Bank Passbook
  • Passport-size Photograph

How to Apply

  1. Visit your nearest bank.
  2. Ask for the Kisan Credit Card application form.
  3. Submit all required documents.
  4. Bank verifies your application.
  5. Receive your Kisan Credit Card after approval.
