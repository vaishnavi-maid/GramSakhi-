Overview

A resume is the first impression you make on an employer. A well-designed resume highlights your education, skills, projects, achievements, and experience, increasing your chances of getting shortlisted for interviews.

Resume Writing Tips

  • Keep your resume clear and professional.
  • Limit the resume to 1–2 pages.
  • Use simple and easy-to-read fonts.
  • Highlight your skills and achievements.
  • Check for spelling and grammar mistakes.
  • Update your resume regularly.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Providing incorrect information.
  • Using an unprofessional email address.
  • Adding unnecessary personal details.
  • Submitting a resume with spelling errors.
  • Using long paragraphs instead of bullet points.
