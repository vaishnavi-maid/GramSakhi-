Scheme Name

Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)

Category

Pregnant & Lactating Women

Description

PMMVY provides financial assistance to pregnant and breastfeeding women to improve maternal health and nutrition.

Eligibility

  • Indian Citizen
  • Pregnant women and lactating mothers
  • Eligible as per government guidelines

Benefits

  • Financial assistance
  • Improved maternal nutrition
  • Support during pregnancy
  • Better healthcare for mother and child

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Mother & Child Protection Card
  • Bank Passbook
  • Identity Proof
  • Passport-size Photo

How to Apply

  1. Visit the nearest Anganwadi Centre or Health Centre.
  2. Fill the PMMVY application form.
  3. Submit the required documents.
  4. Complete verification.
  5. Receive benefits after approval.
