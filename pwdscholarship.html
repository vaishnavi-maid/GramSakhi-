📋 About Disability Scholarships

The Government of India and various State Governments provide scholarship and educational assistance programmes for eligible students with disabilities.

These programmes are intended to help students continue their education and reduce the financial burden associated with studies.

🎯 Main Objective

To encourage students with disabilities to pursue education by providing financial and educational support according to the eligibility conditions of individual scholarship programmes.

🎓 Types of Educational Support

🏫 Pre-Matric Scholarship

Educational assistance for eligible students at the pre-matric level, according to the applicable scheme.

🎓 Post-Matric Scholarship

Financial support for eligible students studying at post-matriculation levels.

🏛️ Higher Education

Scholarships and fellowships may support eligible students pursuing higher education and research.

💻 Educational Assistance

Depending on the programme, assistance may cover items such as maintenance, books, fees or other educational needs.

✅ General Eligibility

  • Applicant must be a person with disability meeting the applicable disability criteria.
  • Applicant must be enrolled in a recognised educational institution, where required.
  • Income limits may apply depending on the scholarship programme.
  • Academic requirements may apply.
  • Other conditions may vary according to the specific scholarship.

🎁 Possible Benefits

  • Financial assistance for eligible students.
  • Support towards tuition or other approved educational expenses, where applicable.
  • Maintenance or study-related assistance under applicable schemes.
  • Support for continuation of education.

📄 Common Documents

  • Disability certificate / UDID.
  • Aadhaar or accepted identity proof.
  • Recent photograph.
  • Previous academic marksheet or certificate.
  • Current admission or bonafide certificate, where required.
  • Income certificate, where applicable.
  • Bank account details.

📝 How to Apply

  1. Identify the scholarship for which you are eligible.
  2. Visit the relevant official scholarship portal.
  3. Register and complete the application form.
  4. Upload the required documents.
  5. Submit the application before the prescribed deadline.
  6. Track the application status through the relevant portal.

⚠️ Important Information

There is no single scholarship with one common eligibility condition for every student with a disability.

Eligibility, income limits, academic conditions, benefits and application deadlines vary between scholarship programmes.

Always check the latest official notification before applying.
