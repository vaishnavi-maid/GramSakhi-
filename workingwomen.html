Scheme Name

Working Women Hostel Scheme

Category

Working Women

Description

This scheme provides safe, affordable, and conveniently located hostel accommodation for working women and women undergoing job training.

Eligibility

  • Working women
  • Women undergoing professional training
  • Single, widowed, divorced, or married women living away from family due to work

Benefits

  • Safe hostel accommodation
  • Affordable rent
  • Secure environment
  • Hostels with basic facilities

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Identity Proof
  • Employment or Training Certificate
  • Passport-size Photograph
  • Address Proof

How to Apply

  1. Visit the nearest Working Women Hostel.
  2. Contact the hostel administration.
  3. Submit the application form and required documents.
  4. Wait for verification and approval.
