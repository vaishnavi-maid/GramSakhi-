📋 About DDRS

The Deendayal Disabled Rehabilitation Scheme (DDRS) is a Central Government scheme that supports voluntary organisations working for the education, rehabilitation and empowerment of persons with disabilities.

The scheme provides grant-in-aid support to eligible organisations for approved projects and rehabilitation services.

🎯 Main Objective

To create an enabling environment for persons with disabilities and support their participation and equal opportunities in society.

👥 Who Can Benefit?

  • Eligible voluntary organisations and non-governmental organisations can receive support for approved projects.
  • Persons with disabilities benefit indirectly through the rehabilitation and support services provided by these organisations.
  • Organisations must meet the prescribed eligibility and registration requirements.

🧩 Major Areas of Support

🎓 Education

Support for projects providing educational services to persons with disabilities.

🧑‍🦽 Rehabilitation

Rehabilitation services designed to improve independence and participation.

💼 Vocational Training

Support for suitable vocational training and skill development activities.

🤝 Community Support

Community-based services that help persons with disabilities participate in society.

⭐ Examples of Services

  • Special education and learning support.
  • Early intervention services.
  • Rehabilitation and therapy-related services under approved projects.
  • Vocational training and skill development.
  • Community-based rehabilitation.
  • Support services for independent living.

✅ Organisation Eligibility

  • The organisation must meet the conditions prescribed under the DDRS guidelines.
  • It should have the required legal status and registration.
  • The organisation should have appropriate infrastructure and qualified personnel for the proposed project.
  • The project must fall within the approved areas of the scheme.

🎁 Key Benefits

  • Grant-in-aid support to eligible organisations for approved projects.
  • Expansion of rehabilitation services.
  • Improved educational and vocational opportunities.
  • Greater participation and independence of persons with disabilities.

📝 How Organisations Can Apply

  1. Ensure the organisation satisfies the DDRS eligibility requirements.
  2. Prepare the required project proposal and supporting documents.
  3. Apply through the prescribed government process or portal.
  4. Submit required financial, registration and project-related documents.
  5. Wait for scrutiny and approval according to the applicable procedure.

⚠️ Important Information

DDRS is primarily a grant-in-aid scheme for eligible organisations rather than a direct cash-benefit scheme for individual persons with disabilities.

Eligibility, project requirements and funding conditions are governed by the latest DDRS guidelines.
