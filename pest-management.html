🌱 Protect Your Crop

Pests and diseases can reduce crop growth and yield. Regular field observation, good farming practices and early identification can help farmers manage crop problems effectively.

🔍 Early Warning Signs

🍃 Leaf Damage

Holes, curling, discoloration, spots or unexpected leaf drop may indicate pest, disease or nutrient problems.

🌱 Plant Wilting

Sudden or gradual wilting can have several causes including diseases, root problems, water stress or pests.

🐛 Visible Insects

Check both sides of leaves, stems, flowers and fruits for insects or eggs.

🍅 Fruit Damage

Holes, rotting, discoloration or abnormal fruit development should be investigated.

🌿 Integrated Pest Management (IPM)

Integrated Pest Management combines different safe and practical approaches instead of depending only on pesticides.


1

👀 Monitor

Regularly inspect plants, leaves, stems, flowers and fruits.

2

🌱 Prevent

Use healthy planting material, suitable crop rotation and good field sanitation.

3

🧹 Remove

Remove heavily infected plant parts or affected plants when appropriate.

4

🐞 Biological Control

Encourage beneficial insects and use approved biological control methods where suitable.

5

🪤 Mechanical Control

Use traps, barriers or physical removal of pests where practical.

6

🧪 Chemical Control

Use registered pesticides only when necessary and according to the product label and expert recommendations.

🛡️ Prevention Tips

🌱 Healthy Seeds

Use quality and certified seeds or healthy planting material from reliable sources.

🌾 Crop Rotation

Where suitable, rotate crops to reduce the buildup of certain pests and diseases.

🧹 Field Hygiene

Remove crop residues and weeds that may harbour pests or diseases.

💧 Proper Irrigation

Avoid unnecessary irrigation and prolonged waterlogging.

🌬️ Proper Spacing

Appropriate plant spacing can improve air circulation and reduce some disease risks.

🔬 Soil Testing

Use soil-test information to support balanced nutrient management and healthy crop growth.

🧪 Safe Pesticide Use

  • Identify the pest or disease before choosing a pesticide.
  • Use only products registered for the intended crop and pest.
  • Read and follow the product label carefully.
  • Use the recommended dose and application method on the label.
  • Wear appropriate protective equipment.
  • Keep children and animals away during application.
  • Observe the required waiting period before harvesting.
  • Do not mix pesticides unless the combination is specifically recommended by an authorised expert or product label.
  • Store pesticides safely in their original, labelled containers.

🌿 Non-Chemical Management

🪤 Traps

Suitable traps can help monitor or reduce certain insect populations.

🐞 Beneficial Insects

Protect natural enemies such as ladybird beetles and other beneficial organisms.

🌱 Resistant Varieties

Where available, choose varieties with appropriate resistance or tolerance.

🧹 Sanitation

Remove and safely manage severely diseased plant material where appropriate.

⚠️ Important:

Not every leaf spot, insect or yellow leaf requires pesticide treatment. Some symptoms can look similar but have different causes, including nutrient deficiency, water stress, weather damage or disease. Correct identification should be done before treatment.
🚫 Never use an unlabelled or banned pesticide.

Do not increase the pesticide dose on your own. Always follow the current product label and local agricultural recommendations. For serious or uncertain crop problems, contact the Agriculture Department, Krishi Vigyan Kendra or a qualified agricultural expert.
