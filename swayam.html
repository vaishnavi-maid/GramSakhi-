Scheme Name

SWAYAM (Study Webs of Active Learning for Young Aspiring Minds)

Category

Students & Lifelong Learners

Description

SWAYAM is the Government of India's online learning platform offering free courses from school level to postgraduate level, including skill-based and professional courses.

Eligibility

  • Open to all Indian citizens
  • No age limit
  • Anyone interested in learning can enroll

Benefits

  • Free online courses
  • Video lectures
  • Study materials
  • Assignments and quizzes
  • Certificates (for eligible courses after examination)

Required Documents

  • Email ID
  • Mobile Number
  • Basic personal details

How to Apply

  1. Visit the SWAYAM Portal.
  2. Create an account.
  3. Select your preferred course.
  4. Enroll for free.
  5. Start learning.
