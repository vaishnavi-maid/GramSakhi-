Ayushman Bharat Health Scheme

Scheme Overview

Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY) is a flagship health insurance scheme that provides eligible families with cashless treatment at empanelled hospitals across India.

Benefits

Eligibility

  • Applicant must be an eligible beneficiary under PM-JAY.
  • Must be an Indian citizen.
  • Families listed in the SECC database or eligible under the scheme.
  • Senior citizens meeting the scheme criteria can avail the benefits.

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Ayushman Bharat Card (if available)
  • Ration Card
  • Identity Proof
  • Address Proof
  • Mobile Number

How to Apply

  1. Visit the nearest Ayushman Bharat help desk or Common Service Centre (CSC).
  2. Check your eligibility.
  3. Submit the required documents.
  4. Complete the verification process.
  5. Receive your Ayushman Bharat Card and use it for cashless treatment.
