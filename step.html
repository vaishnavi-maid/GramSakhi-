Scheme Name

Support to Training and Employment Programme for Women (STEP)

Category

Women Skill Development

Description

The STEP Scheme provides skill development, vocational training, and employment opportunities to women, helping them become financially independent.

Eligibility

  • Indian women aged 16 years and above
  • Women interested in skill development and employment
  • Preference may be given to economically weaker women

Benefits

  • Free skill training
  • Employment opportunities
  • Entrepreneurship support
  • Income generation
  • Self-employment guidance

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Identity Proof
  • Address Proof
  • Passport-size Photograph
  • Bank Passbook

How to Apply

  1. Visit the Women & Child Development Office.
  2. Contact an approved STEP training institute.
  3. Fill out the application form.
  4. Submit the required documents.
  5. Join the training programme after approval.
