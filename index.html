Find government schemes for farmers, students, women, senior citizens and other rural citizens. Check benefits, eligibility, documents and official websites in one place.

🇮🇳 Central Government Schemes

🌾

PM-KISAN

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi

Income support for eligible landholding farmer families.

🎁 Benefits

Eligible farmer families receive financial support under PM-KISAN, subject to applicable rules.

✅ Eligibility

Eligible landholding farmer families meeting the applicable conditions and exclusions.

📄 Documents

Aadhaar, land records, identity and bank-related documents as applicable.

📝 Application

Registration and beneficiary services are available through the official PM-KISAN portal.

🌐 Official Website
🏠

PMAY-G

Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana – Gramin

Housing assistance for eligible rural households.

🎁 Benefits

Assistance for construction of a permanent house for eligible rural households.

✅ Eligibility

Eligibility is determined according to PMAY-G guidelines and beneficiary criteria.

📄 Documents

Identity, household, address and other documents as applicable.

📝 Application

Use the official PMAY-G portal and authorised government process.

🌐 Official Website
🔥

PM Ujjwala Yojana

LPG connection support

Support for eligible households to access clean cooking fuel.

🎁 Benefits

Eligible beneficiaries can receive LPG connection-related support under the scheme.

✅ Eligibility

Eligibility depends on the current PMUY guidelines and beneficiary conditions.

📄 Documents

Aadhaar, address, household and other applicable documents.

📝 Application

Apply through an authorised LPG distributor or official PMUY process.

🌐 Official Website
💰

PM MUDRA Yojana

Support for micro enterprises

Credit support for eligible micro and small business activities.

🎁 Benefits

Eligible entrepreneurs can seek business credit under applicable MUDRA categories.

✅ Eligibility

Eligibility depends on the business, loan category and lending institution.

📄 Documents

Identity, address, business and financial documents as required.

📝 Application

Apply through participating banks, financial institutions or authorised channels.

🌐 Official Website
🏦

PM Jan Dhan Yojana

Financial inclusion

Banking access and financial inclusion for eligible citizens.

🎁 Benefits

Access to basic banking services and other applicable financial inclusion benefits.

✅ Eligibility

Individuals meeting the applicable bank account opening requirements.

📄 Documents

Identity and address documents as required by the bank.

📝 Application

Visit a participating bank branch or use applicable banking channels.

🌐 Official Website
🌱

PM Fasal Bima Yojana

Crop insurance

Crop insurance support against specified risks and losses.

🎁 Benefits

Insurance protection for eligible crops against covered risks according to scheme rules.

✅ Eligibility

Eligible farmers and crops as specified under the applicable guidelines.

📄 Documents

Farmer identity, land/crop records and other required documents.

📝 Application

Apply through the authorised crop insurance and government channels.

🌐 Official Website
