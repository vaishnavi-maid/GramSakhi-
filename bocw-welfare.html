📋 About BOCW Welfare

Building and Other Construction Workers (BOCW) welfare programmes are designed to provide social security and welfare assistance to eligible registered construction workers.

Welfare Boards established under the applicable state framework provide different benefits to registered construction workers and their families.

🎯 Main Objective

To improve the social security, safety, health, education and economic well-being of eligible construction workers and their families.

✅ Who Can Benefit?

  • Eligible building and construction workers.
  • Workers who satisfy the applicable registration requirements.
  • Registered beneficiaries of the concerned Construction Workers Welfare Board.
  • Eligible family members may receive certain benefits according to the applicable welfare scheme.

🎁 Major Welfare Benefits

🎓 Education Support

Financial assistance may be provided for education of eligible workers' children according to applicable rules.

🏥 Medical Assistance

Certain medical and health-related assistance may be available to eligible registered workers.

💍 Marriage Assistance

Eligible beneficiaries may receive financial assistance for specified marriage-related expenses.

🏠 Housing Support

Housing-related assistance may be available under applicable welfare programmes.

🤰 Maternity Support

Eligible women construction workers may receive maternity-related benefits under applicable schemes.

⚰️ Death Assistance

Financial assistance may be provided to eligible families in specified circumstances.

📝 Registration Eligibility

Eligibility requirements vary according to the concerned State/UT's BOCW Welfare Board.

  • Applicant must be engaged in eligible building or construction work.
  • The worker must satisfy the prescribed age and work-period requirements.
  • Required identity and employment-related information must be submitted.
  • Registration with the concerned Welfare Board is generally required to access benefits.

📄 Common Documents

  • Aadhaar card or accepted identity proof.
  • Address proof.
  • Bank account details.
  • Passport-size photographs, where required.
  • Proof of employment/work as required by the Welfare Board.
  • Other documents specified by the concerned State/UT Welfare Board.

📝 How to Register

  • Identify the Construction Workers Welfare Board applicable to your State/UT.
  • Obtain the registration form or use the authorised online registration facility.
  • Enter the required personal and employment information.
  • Upload or submit the required documents.
  • Complete verification and registration as required.

🌿 Maharashtra Construction Workers

In Maharashtra, eligible construction workers can access welfare services through the Maharashtra Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (MBOCWW).

💡 GramSakhi Tip

Before applying for a benefit, check whether your BOCW registration is active and whether you satisfy the eligibility conditions of that particular benefit.

⚠️ Important Information

BOCW benefits are not identical across all States and Union Territories.

Benefit amounts, eligibility conditions, documents and application procedures depend on the applicable Welfare Board and current government rules.

🌐 Official Information

For Maharashtra workers, check the official Maharashtra Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board portal for current registration and welfare benefit information.
