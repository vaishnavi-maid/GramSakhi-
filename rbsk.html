📋 About RBSK

Rashtriya Bal Swasthya Karyakram (RBSK) is a child health screening programme under the National Health Mission. It focuses on early identification and management of health conditions among children.

🎯 Main Purpose

To detect health conditions early and provide appropriate support, treatment or referral to improve the health and development of children.

🎯 Objectives

  • Early identification of health conditions among children.
  • Provide appropriate treatment and follow-up.
  • Refer children requiring specialised care.
  • Reduce the impact of health problems on children's growth and development.
  • Improve access to child healthcare services.

🔎 The 4 Ds of RBSK

1️⃣ Defects at Birth

Identification of congenital conditions present from birth.

2️⃣ Diseases

Identification of selected childhood diseases and health conditions.

3️⃣ Deficiencies

Identification of nutritional and other health deficiencies.

4️⃣ Developmental Delays

Identification of developmental delays and disabilities requiring support.

🩺 Health Screening

Children are screened for specified health conditions through designated public health mechanisms.

  • Physical health screening
  • Growth and development assessment
  • Screening for selected diseases
  • Identification of nutritional deficiencies
  • Developmental and disability screening
  • Referral for further examination when required

👶 Who Can Benefit?

🏫 School Children

Children in the programme's designated school-age groups may receive screening through public health mechanisms.

🏠 Children in the Community

Young children may be screened through community and early childhood care settings covered by the programme.

🏥 Referral & Treatment

  • Children identified with health conditions may be referred for further evaluation.
  • Appropriate treatment may be provided through designated government health facilities.
  • Children requiring specialist care may be referred to higher-level facilities.
  • Follow-up may be provided according to the identified health condition.

📝 How to Access RBSK Services

  • Contact your nearest government health facility.
  • Ask the local ASHA/health worker about child screening services.
  • Contact the child's school or Anganwadi centre where applicable.
  • Follow the referral instructions given by the healthcare team.

⚠️ Important Information

RBSK is a public health screening programme. Screening does not necessarily mean that a child has a particular disease or condition.

Availability of specific services and referral facilities may vary by location.

For current information, contact the nearest government health facility.
