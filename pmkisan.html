Scheme Name

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)

Category

Farmer Welfare Scheme

Description

PM-KISAN provides financial assistance of ₹6,000 per year to eligible farmer families in three equal installments.

Eligibility

  • Indian farmer families
  • Must own eligible agricultural land
  • Must meet government eligibility rules

Benefits

  • ₹6,000 financial assistance per year
  • Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)
  • Three installments of ₹2,000 each

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Bank Passbook
  • Land Records
  • Mobile Number
  • Passport-size Photo

How to Apply

  1. Visit the nearest CSC or Agriculture Office.
  2. Fill out the PM-KISAN application form.
  3. Submit the required documents.
  4. Wait for verification.
  5. Receive benefits directly into your bank account.
