Scheme Name

Rashtriya Gokul Mission (RGM)

Category

Animal Husbandry & Dairy Development

Description

The Rashtriya Gokul Mission aims to improve indigenous cattle breeds, increase milk production, and support livestock farmers through scientific breeding and conservation.

Eligibility

  • Livestock farmers
  • Dairy farmers
  • Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs)
  • Eligible dairy cooperatives

Benefits

  • Improved cattle breeds
  • Higher milk production
  • Veterinary support
  • Artificial insemination services
  • Training for dairy farmers

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Bank Passbook
  • Identity Proof
  • Livestock Details
  • Passport-size Photograph

How to Apply

  1. Visit the nearest Animal Husbandry Office.
  2. Fill out the application form.
  3. Submit the required documents.
  4. Verification by the department.
  5. Receive benefits after approval.
