📋 About the Scheme

Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY) is a maternity benefit programme of the Government of India. It provides financial assistance to eligible women according to the applicable scheme guidelines.

🎯 Main Purpose

To provide maternity-related financial support and encourage appropriate health-seeking behaviour among eligible pregnant and lactating women.

🎯 Objectives

  • Provide partial compensation for wage loss associated with pregnancy and childbirth.
  • Encourage women to seek antenatal and postnatal healthcare.
  • Promote better health and nutrition for mothers and children.
  • Provide financial assistance to eligible beneficiaries under the scheme.

💰 Benefits

💵 Financial Assistance

Eligible beneficiaries can receive maternity-related financial assistance according to current PMMVY provisions.

🤰 Maternal Support

Supports women during pregnancy and the maternity period.

🥗 Nutrition Support

Encourages attention to maternal health and nutrition.

🏥 Healthcare

Encourages eligible women to use appropriate maternal healthcare services.

👩 Eligibility

PMMVY benefits are available to eligible pregnant and lactating women who satisfy the conditions specified under the current scheme guidelines.

  • Eligibility is subject to the current government guidelines.
  • Beneficiary conditions may differ according to applicable rules.
  • Required registration and verification must be completed.
Important: Eligibility categories and benefit conditions can change. GramSakhi users should verify the latest rules before applying.

📄 Documents Generally Required

  • Aadhaar or another accepted identity document
  • Mobile number
  • Bank account details
  • Pregnancy and maternal health records
  • Other documents required during registration

📝 How to Apply

  • Check your eligibility under the current PMMVY guidelines.
  • Contact the appropriate Anganwadi Centre, health facility or designated authority.
  • Provide the required documents and information.
  • Complete beneficiary registration.
  • Complete any required verification.
  • Track the application/benefit status through the applicable government system.

⚠️ Important Information

PMMVY eligibility, benefits, documentation and application procedures may be updated by the Government of India.

Always verify the latest information through an official government source or the concerned Anganwadi/health authority.
