📋 About National AYUSH Mission

The National AYUSH Mission (NAM) is a Government of India initiative that supports the development and strengthening of AYUSH healthcare systems and services.

🎯 Main Purpose

To improve access to quality AYUSH healthcare services and strengthen AYUSH facilities, institutions and related systems.

🌿 AYUSH Systems

🌿 Ayurveda

A traditional Indian system of medicine focusing on health promotion and holistic approaches to wellbeing.

🧘 Yoga

Practices involving physical postures, breathing and related techniques that support wellbeing.

🌱 Unani

A recognised system of traditional medicine practised through qualified healthcare professionals.

🌿 Siddha

A traditional system of medicine with roots in South India.

🌸 Homoeopathy

A recognised system of medicine included within the AYUSH framework.

🎯 Objectives

  • Improve access to AYUSH healthcare services.
  • Strengthen AYUSH health facilities.
  • Support availability of qualified AYUSH healthcare services.
  • Improve infrastructure and related facilities.
  • Promote awareness about AYUSH systems.

🏥 Key Areas of Support

  • Strengthening AYUSH healthcare facilities.
  • Supporting AYUSH services through public healthcare systems.
  • Development of AYUSH infrastructure.
  • Support for availability of medicines and related resources as per programme guidelines.
  • Promotion of preventive and promotive healthcare practices.

👨‍👩‍👧 Who Can Access AYUSH Services?

AYUSH healthcare services may be available to members of the public through government and other authorised AYUSH facilities, depending on local availability.

  • Children
  • Adults
  • Women
  • Senior citizens
  • Other eligible members of the community

📝 How to Access AYUSH Services

  • Visit a government AYUSH healthcare facility.
  • Contact your nearest government health centre and ask about available AYUSH services.
  • Consult a qualified AYUSH healthcare professional.
  • Follow the treatment and health advice provided by the qualified practitioner.

⚠️ Important Information

AYUSH systems should be accessed through qualified and authorised practitioners.

Availability of specific AYUSH services and facilities can vary by location.

Do not stop or replace prescribed medical treatment without consulting an appropriate qualified healthcare professional.
