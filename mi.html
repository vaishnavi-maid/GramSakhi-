📋 About Mission Indradhanush

Mission Indradhanush is an intensified immunization initiative of the Government of India. It aims to improve immunization coverage among children and pregnant women who may have missed routine vaccination.

🎯 Main Purpose

To identify and vaccinate children and pregnant women who are partially vaccinated or unvaccinated, according to the applicable immunization programme.

🎯 Objectives

  • Improve full immunization coverage.
  • Reach children who have missed routine vaccination doses.
  • Reach pregnant women who have missed recommended vaccination.
  • Reduce the risk of vaccine-preventable diseases.
  • Strengthen immunization services in underserved areas.

👶 Target Beneficiaries

👶 Children

Children who are unvaccinated or partially vaccinated may be identified and provided missed doses according to the immunization schedule.

🤰 Pregnant Women

Pregnant women who have missed recommended vaccinations may be reached through immunization activities.

🏘️ Underserved Communities

Special efforts may focus on communities and areas where routine immunization coverage is lower.

🚶 Hard-to-Reach Areas

Immunization activities can be intensified in difficult-to-reach populations and locations.

✅ Key Benefits

  • Helps children receive missed vaccinations.
  • Helps improve protection against vaccine-preventable diseases.
  • Supports vaccination of pregnant women according to the recommended schedule.
  • Helps increase immunization coverage among underserved populations.

📝 How Mission Indradhanush Works

  • Health workers identify children and pregnant women who have missed recommended vaccines.
  • Beneficiaries are informed about vaccination sessions.
  • Vaccines are provided through designated government immunization services.
  • Missed doses are provided according to the appropriate immunization schedule.
  • Follow-up may be carried out for remaining doses.

🏥 How to Access Vaccination

  • Visit the nearest government health facility.
  • Contact an ASHA worker or other local health worker.
  • Contact the nearest Anganwadi Centre where applicable.
  • Ask about upcoming immunization sessions.
  • Carry the child's vaccination record if available.

⚠️ Important Information

Mission Indradhanush is an immunization initiative and vaccination should be provided according to the current National Immunization Schedule and advice of qualified healthcare professionals.

Availability and implementation of special immunization drives may vary by location and government programme schedules.

🌐 Official Information

For official information about Mission Indradhanush and India's immunization programmes, visit the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare.
