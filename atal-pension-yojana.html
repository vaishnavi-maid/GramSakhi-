📋 About Atal Pension Yojana

Atal Pension Yojana (APY) is a government-backed pension scheme designed to provide a regular pension after the subscriber reaches the prescribed age.

The scheme is particularly aimed at providing social-security support to workers who may not have access to a formal pension system.

🎯 Main Objective

To provide old-age income security through a guaranteed minimum pension based on the subscriber's chosen pension amount and contributions, subject to scheme rules.

✅ Who Can Join?

  • Indian citizens generally between 18 and 40 years of age can join APY, subject to applicable rules.
  • The subscriber should have a savings bank account or eligible post-office savings account.
  • Contributions are made periodically through the linked account.
  • From 1 October 2022, income-tax payers are not eligible to open a new APY account, subject to applicable government rules.
Important: Eligibility rules may change. Check the latest APY guidelines before opening an account.

💰 Pension Options

APY provides different guaranteed minimum monthly pension options after the subscriber reaches 60 years, subject to scheme conditions.

₹1,000 Pension

Guaranteed minimum monthly pension option.

Guaranteed minimum monthly pension option.

₹3,000 Pension

Guaranteed minimum monthly pension option.

₹4,000 Pension

Guaranteed minimum monthly pension option.

₹5,000 Pension

Highest guaranteed minimum monthly pension option under APY.

🎁 Key Benefits

  • Guaranteed minimum monthly pension after the subscriber reaches 60 years, according to the selected option.
  • Pension is payable to the subscriber during their lifetime, subject to scheme rules.
  • After the subscriber's death, the spouse is entitled to the same pension under applicable rules.
  • After the death of both subscriber and spouse, the pension wealth is returned to the nominee according to scheme provisions.

💳 Contribution

The amount a subscriber contributes depends on factors such as:

  • Age at the time of joining.
  • Selected pension amount.
  • Contribution frequency.

💡 Regular Contribution

Contributions are generally made monthly, quarterly or half-yearly through auto-debit from the subscriber's account.

📄 Required Information

  • Savings bank or eligible post-office account.
  • Aadhaar details, where required.
  • Mobile number, where required.
  • Nominee details.
  • Spouse details, where applicable.

📝 How to Apply

  • Visit a participating bank or post-office.
  • Request an Atal Pension Yojana registration form.
  • Select the desired pension amount.
  • Provide the required personal, nominee and account information.
  • Give consent for contribution auto-debit.
  • Submit the application to the participating institution.

⚠️ Important Information

APY is a long-term pension scheme. Contributions should be maintained according to the applicable schedule.

Pension amount, contribution requirements, eligibility and withdrawal provisions are governed by the latest official APY rules.
