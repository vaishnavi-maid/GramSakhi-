📋 About PMAY-G

Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G) is a Government of India rural housing programme aimed at providing assistance to eligible rural households for construction of a pucca house with basic amenities.

🎯 Main Objective

To help eligible rural households living in inadequate housing conditions obtain a pucca house with basic amenities.

👨‍👩‍👧 Eligibility

Beneficiary selection is based on the applicable government criteria and identification process for eligible rural households.

  • Eligible rural households requiring housing assistance.
  • Households identified through the prescribed PMAY-G beneficiary selection process.
  • Priority is given according to applicable government guidelines.
Important: Eligibility should be verified through the official PMAY-G system or the concerned Gram Panchayat.

💰 Key Benefits

  • Financial assistance for construction of a pucca house.
  • Support for basic housing amenities as provided under applicable convergence programmes.
  • Wage support may be linked with MGNREGA according to applicable provisions.
  • Assistance for sanitation facilities may be provided through applicable government programmes.

🏠 Housing Assistance

🏡 Pucca House

Assistance is provided to eligible rural households for construction of a pucca house.

🚰 Basic Amenities

The programme works with other government schemes to support basic amenities.

🚽 Sanitation

Sanitation support may be linked with relevant government programmes.

💼 Wage Support

Eligible beneficiaries may receive wage support through applicable MGNREGA provisions.

📄 Documents / Information

  • Aadhaar or other approved identification document, as applicable.
  • Bank account details for direct benefit transfer where applicable.
  • Beneficiary and household information.
  • Other documents required by the concerned authorities.

📝 How to Check / Access the Scheme

  • Contact your Gram Panchayat.
  • Ask the concerned officials about PMAY-G beneficiary status.
  • Verify your details through the official PMAY-G system.
  • Follow the instructions provided by the concerned government authority.

⚠️ Important Information

PMAY-G assistance and beneficiary selection are subject to government guidelines.

The amount and conditions of assistance may be revised by the Government from time to time.

Always verify the latest information through official government sources or your Gram Panchayat.

🌐 Official Information

For official information about PMAY-G, beneficiary details and programme updates, visit the official PMAY-G portal.
