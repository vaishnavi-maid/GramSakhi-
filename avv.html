📋 About the Scheme

The Ayushman Vay Vandana Card provides health coverage under the Ayushman Bharat - Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB PM-JAY) for eligible senior citizens aged 70 years and above.

🎯 Main Benefit

Eligible senior citizens aged 70 years and above can receive health coverage of up to ₹5 lakh per year, subject to the applicable PM-JAY rules.

👵 Eligibility

  • Senior citizens aged 70 years and above are covered under the expanded AB PM-JAY provision.
  • Eligibility is not based on family income for the 70+ senior-citizen expansion.
  • Aadhaar-based e-KYC is required for enrolment.
Important: The exact process and applicable conditions should be verified through the official PM-JAY system.

💰 Key Benefits

🏥 Health Cover

Up to ₹5 lakh health cover per year for eligible senior citizens, according to applicable PM-JAY rules.

🏨 Hospitalisation

Provides coverage for eligible secondary and tertiary hospitalisation services at empanelled hospitals.

💳 Cashless Treatment

Eligible beneficiaries can access covered treatment through the PM-JAY cashless system at empanelled hospitals.

👴 Senior Citizen Support

Provides additional health protection for senior citizens aged 70 years and above.

📄 Documents / Information

  • Aadhaar card
  • Aadhaar-linked mobile number where applicable
  • Basic beneficiary information
  • Any additional information requested during enrolment or verification

📝 How to Get the Card

  • Check eligibility through the official PM-JAY system.
  • Complete the required Aadhaar-based e-KYC.
  • Follow the enrolment process provided by the authorised PM-JAY system or centre.
  • Download or obtain the Ayushman card after successful verification, as applicable.

🏥 Where Can Treatment Be Taken?

Covered treatment can be taken at hospitals empanelled under the applicable PM-JAY network.

  • Government hospitals participating in PM-JAY
  • Empanelled private hospitals

⚠️ Important Information

The Ayushman Vay Vandana Card is linked to the AB PM-JAY expansion for senior citizens aged 70 years and above.

Coverage, package availability and hospital empanelment are subject to the applicable government rules.

Always verify the latest information through the official PM-JAY portal.
