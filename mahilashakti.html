Scheme Name

Mahila Shakti Kendra (MSK)

Category

Women Empowerment

Description

Mahila Shakti Kendra empowers rural women through skill development, digital literacy, employment support, health awareness, and legal guidance.

Eligibility

  • Indian women
  • Priority for rural women
  • Women seeking skill development or support services

Benefits

  • Skill development training
  • Digital literacy
  • Employment guidance
  • Legal awareness
  • Health and nutrition awareness

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Identity Proof
  • Address Proof
  • Passport-size Photograph
  • Mobile Number

How to Apply

  1. Visit the nearest Mahila Shakti Kendra.
  2. Contact the District Women & Child Development Office.
  3. Fill out the application form.
  4. Submit the required documents.
