📝 How to Apply for an Opportunity

  • Read the official opportunity details.
  • Check eligibility requirements.
  • Note the application deadline.
  • Prepare your resume and documents.
  • Complete the application carefully.
  • Save the application confirmation.

⚠️ Opportunity Safety Tips

  • Verify the organisation before applying.
  • Use official websites whenever possible.
  • Be careful with suspicious offers.
  • Do not share passwords or OTPs.
  • Never pay money for a suspicious job offer.
  • Read the terms and requirements carefully.
💡 Important: Opportunities, eligibility, deadlines and application requirements can change. Always verify the latest details on the official website before applying.
