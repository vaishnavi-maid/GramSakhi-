Scheme Name

Beti Bachao Beti Padhao (BBBP)

Category

Women & Girl Child

Description

This scheme aims to improve the child sex ratio, promote girls' education, and empower women in society.

Eligibility

  • Girl child and her family
  • Indian citizens

Benefits

  • Promotes girls' education
  • Creates awareness about gender equality
  • Supports welfare of the girl child

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Birth Certificate of Girl Child
  • Residence Proof
  • Passport-size Photo

How to Apply

  1. Visit the nearest Anganwadi, District Office or Women & Child Development Office.
  2. Follow the instructions provided by the concerned authority.
