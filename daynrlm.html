Scheme Name

Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana – National Rural Livelihoods Mission (DAY-NRLM)

Category

Women & Self Help Groups (SHGs)

Description

DAY-NRLM helps rural women become financially independent by promoting Self Help Groups (SHGs), skill development, entrepreneurship, and access to loans.

Eligibility

  • Women from rural households
  • Members of Self Help Groups (SHGs)
  • Eligible as per government guidelines

Benefits

  • Formation of Self Help Groups
  • Skill development and training
  • Financial assistance
  • Bank linkage and loans
  • Support for self-employment

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Address Proof
  • Bank Passbook
  • Passport-size Photograph
  • SHG Details (if applicable)

How to Apply

  1. Visit the Gram Panchayat or Block Development Office.
  2. Contact the local NRLM office.
  3. Complete the registration process.
  4. Join or form a Self Help Group.
