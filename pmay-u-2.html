📋 About PMAY-U 2.0

Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Urban 2.0 is a Government of India housing programme designed to provide housing assistance to eligible urban families.

The scheme supports eligible beneficiaries through different housing assistance options according to their requirements and applicable government guidelines.

🎯 Main Objective

To support eligible urban families in constructing, purchasing or obtaining affordable housing.

👨‍👩‍👧 Eligibility

  • Eligible urban families belonging to Economically Weaker Section (EWS), Low Income Group (LIG) or Middle Income Group (MIG), as applicable.
  • Beneficiaries must satisfy the conditions applicable to the selected PMAY-U 2.0 vertical.
  • The applicant and family must meet the applicable ownership and other scheme conditions.
Important: Eligibility differs according to the PMAY-U 2.0 component. Always verify the latest criteria before applying.

🏠 Four Main Components

🏗️ 1. BLC

Beneficiary Led Construction supports eligible beneficiaries in constructing houses on their own available land.

🏢 2. AHP

Affordable Housing in Partnership supports affordable housing projects for eligible beneficiaries.

🏘️ 3. ARH

Affordable Rental Housing supports affordable rental housing for eligible urban groups.

💰 4. ISS

Interest Subsidy Scheme provides interest subsidy support on eligible home loans, subject to applicable conditions.

💰 Key Benefits

  • Housing assistance for eligible urban families.
  • Support for construction or purchase of eligible houses.
  • Affordable rental housing support through the applicable ARH component.
  • Interest subsidy support through the applicable ISS component.
  • Government assistance is subject to the applicable component, eligibility and scheme guidelines.

📄 Common Documents / Information

  • Aadhaar or other approved identification, as applicable.
  • Bank account details where required.
  • Income-related documents, where applicable.
  • Address and family details.
  • Property or land documents where required for the selected component.
  • Any additional documents required by the concerned authority or lender.

📝 How to Apply

  • Identify the PMAY-U 2.0 component applicable to your situation.
  • Check eligibility and required documents.
  • Apply through the prescribed PMAY-U 2.0 process or concerned urban local body.
  • Submit the required information and documents.
  • Track the application according to the available government process.

⚠️ Important Information

PMAY-U 2.0 benefits, eligibility, assistance amounts and conditions depend on the applicable component and current government guidelines.

Applicants should verify the latest information through the official PMAY-U 2.0 portal or their concerned Urban Local Body.

🌐 Official Information

For current information about PMAY-U 2.0, eligibility, application procedures and scheme components, visit the official PMAY Urban portal.
