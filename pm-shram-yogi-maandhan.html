📋 About the Scheme

Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maandhan (PM-SYM) is a Government of India pension scheme for eligible workers in the unorganised sector.

The scheme is designed to provide social security and a regular pension after the eligible subscriber reaches the prescribed age.

🎯 Main Objective

To provide financial security and pension support to eligible unorganised workers during their old age.

👷 Eligibility

  • Applicant should generally be an unorganised worker.
  • The applicable age at entry is generally between 18 and 40 years.
  • Applicant should satisfy the prescribed income conditions.
  • Applicant should not be covered under specified organised-sector social-security schemes such as EPFO, ESIC or NPS, subject to scheme rules.
Important: Eligibility conditions should be checked according to the latest PM-SYM guidelines before enrolment.

💰 Key Benefits

  • Assured monthly pension of ₹3,000 after reaching the prescribed age, subject to scheme conditions.
  • Government provides a matching contribution to the subscriber's contribution according to the scheme rules.
  • Provides social-security support during old age.
  • Eligible spouse may receive family pension according to applicable rules.

👥 Examples of Unorganised Workers

🧱 Construction Workers

Workers engaged in construction and related activities.

🧹 Domestic Workers

Eligible domestic workers can be covered subject to scheme conditions.

🛺 Street & Transport Workers

Eligible drivers, helpers and other unorganised workers.

👨‍🌾 Agricultural Workers

Eligible agricultural and other unorganised workers.

💳 Contribution

The subscriber makes a monthly contribution based on their age at the time of joining the scheme.

The Central Government contributes an equal matching amount according to the applicable PM-SYM rules.

Example: The exact monthly contribution depends on the subscriber's age when joining. Check the official contribution chart before enrolment.

📄 Documents Required

  • Aadhaar card.
  • Savings bank account or Jan-Dhan account details.
  • Mobile number.
  • Nominee details.
  • Other information required during enrolment.

📝 How to Enrol

  • Visit a Common Service Centre (CSC).
  • Carry Aadhaar and bank account details.
  • Provide the required information to the enrolment operator.
  • Complete the required authentication and enrolment process.
  • Pay the applicable subscriber contribution.

⚠️ Important Information

PM-SYM is a contributory pension scheme. The monthly contribution depends on the age at which the worker joins.

Always verify the latest eligibility, contribution amount and scheme rules before enrolling.
