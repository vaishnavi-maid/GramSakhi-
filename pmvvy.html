Pradhan Mantri Vaya Vandana Yojana

Scheme Overview

Pradhan Mantri Vaya Vandana Yojana (PMVVY) is a pension scheme managed by LIC that provides senior citizens with a guaranteed pension and assured returns for financial security after retirement.

Benefits

Eligibility

  • Applicant must be 60 years of age or above.
  • Must be an Indian citizen.
  • The scheme is available through LIC.
  • Applicant should meet the investment conditions prescribed by LIC.

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • PAN Card
  • Age Proof
  • Address Proof
  • Bank Account Details
  • Passport Size Photograph

How to Apply

  1. Visit the nearest LIC branch or the official LIC website.
  2. Fill in the PMVVY application form.
  3. Attach the required documents.
  4. Submit the application along with the investment amount.
  5. After verification, your pension policy will be issued.
