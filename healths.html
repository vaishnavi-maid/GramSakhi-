🌱 Daily Health Tips

  • Drink safe and clean water.
  • Eat a balanced and nutritious diet.
  • Wash your hands regularly with soap and water.
  • Keep your home and surroundings clean.
  • Stay physically active.
  • Get adequate sleep.
  • Take medicines only as advised by a qualified healthcare professional.
  • Seek medical advice when symptoms are serious, persistent or worsening.
