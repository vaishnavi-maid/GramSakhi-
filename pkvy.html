Scheme Name

Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (PKVY)

Category

Organic Farming Scheme

Description

PKVY encourages farmers to adopt organic farming methods by providing financial assistance, training, certification, and marketing support for organic produce.

Eligibility

  • Indian farmers
  • Farmer groups or clusters interested in organic farming
  • Eligible agricultural land owners

Benefits

  • Financial assistance for organic farming
  • Training and awareness programs
  • Organic certification support
  • Improved soil health
  • Better market opportunities for organic products

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Land Records
  • Bank Passbook
  • Mobile Number
  • Passport-size Photograph

How to Apply

  1. Visit the nearest Agriculture Department office.
  2. Apply through your local agriculture officer.
  3. Join an approved organic farming cluster.
  4. Submit the required documents.
  5. Receive training and financial assistance after approval.
