Scheme Name

DigiLocker

Category

Students & Citizens

Description

DigiLocker is a Government of India digital document wallet that allows users to securely store, access and share official documents such as Aadhaar, Driving Licence, Mark Sheets and Certificates.

Eligibility

  • Indian Citizen
  • Valid Mobile Number
  • Aadhaar (recommended)

Benefits

  • Free digital document storage
  • Secure cloud storage
  • Access documents anytime
  • Paperless verification
  • Easy sharing of certificates

Required Documents

  • Mobile Number
  • Aadhaar Card (optional but recommended)
  • Email ID

How to Apply

  1. Visit DigiLocker.
  2. Register using your mobile number.
  3. Verify OTP.
  4. Link Aadhaar (optional).
  5. Start saving and accessing your documents.
