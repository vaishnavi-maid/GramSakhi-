Career Planning Tips

  • Identify your interests and strengths.
  • Set clear short-term and long-term career goals.
  • Research different career opportunities.
  • Improve communication and technical skills.
  • Gain practical experience through internships and projects.
  • Continue learning through online courses and certifications.
