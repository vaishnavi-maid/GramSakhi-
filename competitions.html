💻 What is a Hackathon?

A hackathon is an event where individuals or teams work within a limited period to create a solution, prototype or project for a given problem.

  • Usually team-based.
  • Focuses on problem solving and innovation.
  • May involve coding, design, business or research.
  • Projects are usually presented to judges.

⭐ Benefits of Participating

  • Improve technical and problem-solving skills.
  • Build real-world projects.
  • Improve teamwork and communication.
  • Strengthen your resume and portfolio.
  • Meet other students and professionals.
  • Get opportunities to win prizes and recognition.

Students interested in creativity and design can participate in UI/UX, graphic design, poster, presentation and other design challenges.

👥 How to Build a Hackathon Team

  • Choose members with different skills.
  • Include technical and non-technical skills.
  • Clearly divide responsibilities.
  • Communicate regularly.
  • Practice presenting the project together.

🛠️ How to Prepare for a Hackathon

  • Understand the problem statement carefully.
  • Research the existing solutions.
  • Identify your target users.
  • Build a practical solution or prototype.
  • Test the project before submission.
  • Prepare a clear presentation.
  • Practice your pitch.

🏅 Common Judging Criteria

The judging criteria vary by competition. Common factors may include:

  • Innovation
  • Problem understanding
  • Technical implementation
  • Practical feasibility
  • Social or business impact
  • User experience
  • Presentation quality

📜 Certificates & Achievements

Depending on the organiser, participants may receive participation certificates, winning certificates, prizes or other recognition.

Keep genuine certificates and achievement records safely for your portfolio and resume.

⚠️ Safety Tips

  • Check the organiser before registering.
  • Read the official rules carefully.
  • Check registration deadlines.
  • Be careful with suspicious links and offers.
  • Do not share sensitive information unnecessarily.
  • Verify prize and certificate claims from official sources.
💡 Important: Competition dates, rules, eligibility, registration fees, prizes and problem statements can change. Always check the organiser's official website before registering.
