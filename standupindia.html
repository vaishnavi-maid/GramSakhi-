Scheme Name

Stand Up India Scheme

Category

Women Entrepreneurship

Description

Stand Up India helps women entrepreneurs establish new businesses by providing bank loans for greenfield enterprises in manufacturing, services, trading, and agriculture-related activities.

Eligibility

  • Women entrepreneurs aged 18 years or above
  • Indian citizens
  • Starting a new (greenfield) business

Benefits

  • Bank loans from ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore
  • Support for starting a business
  • Business guidance and mentoring
  • Encourages women entrepreneurship

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • PAN Card
  • Business Plan
  • Address Proof
  • Bank Account Details
  • Passport-size Photograph

How to Apply

  1. Visit a participating bank branch or the Stand Up India portal.
  2. Submit the application form.
  3. Provide the required documents.
  4. Complete bank verification.
  5. Receive loan approval if eligible.
