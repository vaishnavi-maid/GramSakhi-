🎤 Interview Preparation

Overview

Interview preparation helps candidates build confidence, improve communication skills, and answer interview questions effectively. Proper preparation increases the chances of getting selected for a job.

Common Interview Questions

Interview Tips

  • Research the company before attending the interview.
  • Practice common HR and technical questions.
  • Maintain confidence and a positive attitude.
  • Speak clearly and politely.
  • Carry all required documents.
  • Reach the interview venue on time.

Dress Code & Professional Etiquette

  • Wear clean and formal clothes.
  • Maintain good personal hygiene.
  • Make eye contact while speaking.
  • Listen carefully before answering.
  • Thank the interviewer at the end.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Arriving late.
  • Giving incorrect information.
  • Using a mobile phone during the interview.
  • Speaking negatively about previous employers.
  • Lack of confidence and preparation.
