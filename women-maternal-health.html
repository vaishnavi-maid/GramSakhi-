Women & Maternal Health Schemes

Find government programmes that support women during pregnancy, childbirth and the postnatal period, along with nutrition and maternal healthcare services.

Maternity Assistance

🤰 Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)

Purpose: Provides maternity-related financial assistance to eligible women according to scheme guidelines.

Focus Areas:

  • Pregnancy and maternity support
  • Nutrition and health support
  • Financial assistance for eligible beneficiaries

Eligibility: Eligible women as specified under the current scheme guidelines.

Safe Motherhood

🏥 Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY)

Purpose: Promotes institutional delivery and aims to reduce maternal and newborn mortality.

Support:

  • Encourages institutional delivery
  • Support for eligible pregnant women
  • Links beneficiaries with public health services
Know More
Maternal Healthcare

🩺 Janani Shishu Suraksha Karyakram (JSSK)

Purpose: Provides free and cashless services to eligible pregnant women and sick newborns in public health institutions, as per programme provisions.

Services may include:

  • Free delivery services
  • Free medicines and diagnostics
  • Referral transport support
  • Post-delivery care
Know More
Maternal Care

❤️ Pradhan Mantri Surakshit Matritva Abhiyan (PMSMA)

Purpose: Provides assured antenatal care to pregnant women at designated government health facilities.

Focus:

  • Antenatal check-ups
  • Identification of high-risk pregnancies
  • Specialist consultation where available
  • Maternal health awareness

Nutrition

🥗 POSHAN Abhiyaan

Purpose: Improves nutrition outcomes for women, children and other vulnerable groups.

Focus Areas:

  • Maternal nutrition
  • Awareness about healthy nutrition
  • Prevention of malnutrition
  • Improving nutrition-related outcomes
