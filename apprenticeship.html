🎓 Apprenticeships

Overview

Apprenticeship programs help students and job seekers gain practical work experience while learning industry skills. These programs are offered by government organizations, public sector companies, and private industries across India.

Benefits

Eligibility

  • Must be an Indian citizen.
  • Should have passed 10th, 12th, ITI, Diploma, or Graduation (depending on the apprenticeship).
  • Must meet the age criteria specified by the employer.
  • Should possess the required technical or vocational qualification.

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Educational Certificates
  • Passport Size Photograph
  • Bank Passbook
  • Mobile Number & Email ID
  • Caste Certificate (if applicable)

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official Apprenticeship Portal.
  2. Register using your mobile number and email.
  3. Complete your profile with educational details.
  4. Search for available apprenticeship opportunities.
  5. Apply to your preferred organization and wait for selection.
