📋 About e-Shram

e-Shram is a national database of unorganised workers created by the Government of India.

Eligible unorganised workers can register on the e-Shram portal and receive a Universal Account Number (UAN).

🎯 Main Objective

To create a comprehensive database of unorganised workers and help them access social-security and welfare benefits for which they are eligible.

👷 Who Can Register?

e-Shram is intended for eligible workers in the unorganised sector.

👨‍🌾 Agricultural Workers

Eligible agricultural workers and labourers.

🧱 Construction Workers

Workers involved in construction and related activities.

🧹 Domestic Workers

Eligible domestic workers employed in households.

🛺 Street Vendors

Eligible street vendors and other self-employed workers.

✅ Basic Eligibility

  • Worker should be engaged in the unorganised sector.
  • Generally, the worker should be between 16 and 59 years of age for e-Shram registration.
  • Worker should not be a member of EPFO or ESIC, subject to applicable rules.
  • Worker should provide the required identification and personal details.
Important: Registration on e-Shram does not automatically mean that a worker is eligible for every government welfare scheme. Each scheme has its own eligibility conditions.

🎁 Benefits of e-Shram Registration

  • Provides the worker with a permanent Universal Account Number (UAN).
  • Helps create a national record of unorganised workers.
  • Can help facilitate access to eligible social-security and welfare schemes.
  • Helps the government identify and support unorganised workers during welfare interventions.

📄 Documents / Details Required

  • Aadhaar number.
  • Aadhaar-linked mobile number, where applicable.
  • Bank account details.
  • Personal and occupation-related details.
  • Other information requested during registration.

📝 How to Register

  • Visit the official e-Shram portal.
  • Select the registration option.
  • Enter the required mobile and Aadhaar details.
  • Complete Aadhaar authentication.
  • Enter personal, occupation and bank account information.
  • Submit the registration details.
  • Download or save the e-Shram card/UAN details after successful registration.

🔢 What is UAN?

UAN means Universal Account Number.

It is a unique number associated with an eligible worker's e-Shram registration.

💡 Remember

Keep your e-Shram/UAN details safe because they may be required when accessing services connected with the registration.

⚠️ Important Information

e-Shram is primarily a registration and database platform. The availability of any particular benefit depends on the eligibility requirements of the concerned government scheme.

Always check the latest information on the official e-Shram portal before applying for any benefit.
