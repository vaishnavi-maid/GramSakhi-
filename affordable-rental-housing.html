📋 About Affordable Rental Housing

Affordable Rental Housing Complexes (ARHCs) are intended to provide affordable rental accommodation to eligible urban residents, particularly people who need rental housing near employment and livelihood opportunities.

The initiative is especially useful for migrants, workers, students and other economically vulnerable urban groups who may not own a house.

🎯 Main Objective

To provide affordable and accessible rental housing for eligible urban households and workers.

👨‍👩‍👧 Who Can Benefit?

  • Migrant workers.
  • Construction workers.
  • Industrial workers.
  • Street vendors.
  • Domestic workers.
  • Students and other eligible urban residents.
  • Economically weaker and low-income groups, subject to applicable rules.

💰 Key Benefits

  • Access to affordable rental accommodation.
  • Housing closer to employment opportunities.
  • Helps reduce the housing burden on low-income urban residents.
  • Rental housing can provide an alternative for people who cannot immediately purchase a house.
  • Basic civic amenities may be provided according to the applicable housing complex.

🏘️ Main Features

🏠 Rental Housing

Provides housing on a rental basis instead of requiring beneficiaries to purchase a home.

💵 Affordable Rent

The programme aims to make rental housing more affordable for eligible urban groups.

👷 Workers

Supports workers and migrants who need accommodation near their workplaces.

🏙️ Urban Areas

The initiative focuses on rental housing requirements in urban areas.

🔄 How It Works

  • Affordable rental housing complexes are developed or made available through the applicable government framework.
  • Eligible people can look for an available rental unit in an ARHC.
  • The applicant completes the required registration or rental procedure.
  • Rent and other conditions depend on the particular housing complex and applicable rules.

📄 Common Documents / Information

  • Aadhaar or another accepted identity document.
  • Mobile number.
  • Address and personal details.
  • Employment or income-related information, where required.
  • Any additional documents requested by the housing provider or authority.

⚠️ Important Information

Availability of rental units, rent, eligibility, documents and application procedures can differ between housing complexes.

Users should check the latest availability and conditions through the official housing authority or concerned local authority.
