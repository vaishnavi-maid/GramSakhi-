📋 About PM SVANidhi

PM Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi (PM SVANidhi) is a Government of India scheme designed to support eligible street vendors.

The scheme provides institutional credit support to eligible street vendors to help them restart, maintain or expand their vending activities.

🎯 Main Objective

To provide affordable working-capital credit to eligible street vendors and support their livelihoods.

🛺 Who Can Benefit?

The scheme is intended for eligible street vendors operating in urban areas and other areas covered under the applicable guidelines.

🥬 Vegetable Vendors

Vendors selling vegetables and other everyday products.

🍲 Food Vendors

Eligible vendors selling food and refreshments.

🧺 Small Traders

Eligible street-based traders and small sellers.

🛠️ Service Vendors

Eligible street vendors providing services.

✅ Eligibility

  • Applicant should be an eligible street vendor covered under the scheme.
  • Vendor should satisfy the applicable vending-related requirements.
  • Required identification and vending information must be provided.
  • Eligibility may depend on the applicable survey, certificate of vending, Letter of Recommendation or other recognised process.
Important: Eligibility and lending conditions can depend on the current PM SVANidhi guidelines and the applicant's circumstances.

💰 Working Capital Loan

PM SVANidhi provides collateral-free working capital loans to eligible street vendors, subject to applicable scheme conditions.

  • Loan support can help vendors meet their working-capital requirements.
  • Successful repayment can make the vendor eligible for higher loan cycles under applicable rules.
  • Digital transactions can provide additional incentives according to scheme provisions.

🎁 Key Benefits

  • Access to formal working-capital credit.
  • No collateral is generally required for eligible scheme loans.
  • Interest subsidy may be available for eligible timely loan repayment according to scheme rules.
  • Digital transaction incentives may be available to eligible vendors.
  • Higher loan amounts may be available in subsequent cycles for eligible vendors who meet repayment conditions.

📄 Documents / Information

  • Aadhaar or accepted identity document.
  • Mobile number.
  • Bank account details.
  • Certificate of Vending / identity card, where applicable.
  • Letter of Recommendation, where applicable.
  • Other documents requested during the application process.

📝 How to Apply

  • Visit the concerned Urban Local Body, bank, lending institution or authorised application channel.
  • Provide the required identification and vending-related details.
  • Submit the application for working-capital credit.
  • Complete verification by the concerned authorities/lender.
  • After approval, the loan is processed by the lending institution.

⚠️ Important Information

Loan amount, repayment conditions, interest subsidy, digital incentives and other benefits are subject to the latest PM SVANidhi guidelines.

Always verify the current rules and eligibility before applying.
