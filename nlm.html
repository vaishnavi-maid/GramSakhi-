Scheme Name

National Livestock Mission (NLM)

Category

Livestock Development

Description

The National Livestock Mission promotes sustainable livestock development by supporting sheep, goat, pig, poultry, and fodder development. It helps improve farmers' income through better animal husbandry practices.

Eligibility

  • Livestock farmers
  • Individual entrepreneurs
  • Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs)
  • Self Help Groups (SHGs)

Benefits

  • Financial assistance for livestock farming
  • Support for poultry, sheep and goat farming
  • Fodder development assistance
  • Skill development and training
  • Income generation opportunities

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Identity Proof
  • Bank Passbook
  • Project Report (if applicable)
  • Passport-size Photograph

How to Apply

  1. Visit the nearest Animal Husbandry Office.
  2. Fill out the application form.
  3. Submit the required documents.
  4. Verification by the department.
  5. Receive benefits after approval.
