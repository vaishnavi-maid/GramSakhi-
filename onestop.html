Scheme Name

One Stop Centre (OSC) Scheme

Category

Women Safety

Description

The One Stop Centre Scheme provides integrated support and assistance to women affected by violence, including medical aid, legal assistance, counselling, police support and temporary shelter.

Eligibility

  • Women of any age facing violence or abuse
  • Indian citizens

Benefits

  • Medical assistance
  • Police support
  • Legal aid
  • Counselling services
  • Temporary shelter

Required Documents

  • Identity Proof (if available)
  • Complaint details (if applicable)
  • Medical records (if available)

How to Apply

  1. Visit the nearest One Stop Centre.
  2. Call the Women Helpline.
  3. Contact the District Women & Child Development Office.
  4. Receive immediate assistance.
