📋 About POSHAN Abhiyaan

POSHAN Abhiyaan is a Government of India initiative focused on improving nutrition outcomes and addressing malnutrition among children, pregnant women and lactating mothers.

🎯 Main Purpose

To improve nutritional outcomes through convergence, awareness, community participation and better implementation of nutrition-related services.

🎯 Objectives

  • Improve nutrition outcomes among children.
  • Improve maternal nutrition and health.
  • Create awareness about healthy nutrition practices.
  • Encourage community participation in preventing malnutrition.
  • Support convergence of nutrition-related government services.

👨‍👩‍👧 Target Beneficiaries

👶 Children

Focus on improving nutrition and healthy growth among children.

🤰 Pregnant Women

Promotes awareness and support for better maternal nutrition during pregnancy.

🤱 Lactating Mothers

Encourages appropriate nutrition and health practices during the breastfeeding period.

👩 Women & Families

Promotes awareness about balanced diets, hygiene and healthy nutrition practices.

🥗 Key Areas

  • Maternal nutrition
  • Child nutrition
  • Prevention of malnutrition
  • Nutrition awareness
  • Healthy dietary practices
  • Community participation

🏥 Nutrition-Related Support

Nutrition services are delivered through various government programmes and community-level mechanisms.

  • Nutrition education and awareness
  • Growth monitoring of children
  • Promotion of appropriate infant and young child feeding practices
  • Counselling for pregnant and lactating women
  • Identification and referral of children requiring additional care

📝 How to Access Nutrition Services

  • Visit your nearest Anganwadi Centre.
  • Contact an Anganwadi Worker or ASHA worker.
  • Contact your nearest government health facility.
  • Ask about available nutrition services, counselling and growth monitoring.

⚠️ Important Information

Nutrition services and eligibility for specific benefits may depend on the applicable government programme and local implementation.

For current information about available services, contact your nearest Anganwadi Centre or government health facility.
