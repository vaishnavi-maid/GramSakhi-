Assistance to Persons with Disabilities for Purchase/Fitting of Aids and Appliances

📋 About the ADIP Scheme

The ADIP Scheme provides assistance to eligible persons with disabilities for obtaining suitable aids and appliances.

These assistive devices are intended to help persons with disabilities improve their independence, mobility and ability to participate in daily activities.

🎯 Main Objective

To provide modern and appropriate assistive devices to eligible persons with disabilities and improve their physical, social and economic independence.

✅ Eligibility

  • The applicant should be a person with a disability covered under the applicable guidelines.
  • The applicant must satisfy the prescribed disability-related conditions.
  • The applicant must satisfy the applicable income criteria.
  • A valid disability certificate or UDID may be required.
  • Other conditions prescribed under the latest ADIP guidelines must be fulfilled.

🦽 Types of Aids & Appliances

🦽 Mobility Aids

Wheelchairs, tricycles and other mobility-related assistive devices.

🦻 Hearing Aids

Suitable hearing-related assistive devices for eligible beneficiaries.

🦿 Prosthetic Devices

Prosthetic and orthotic devices may be provided according to assessment and eligibility.

👁️ Visual Aids

Assistive devices that support persons with visual disabilities.

🎁 Key Benefits

  • Assistance towards eligible aids and appliances.
  • Improved mobility and independence.
  • Support for education and employment through appropriate assistive devices.
  • Support for improved participation in everyday activities.

📄 Commonly Required Documents

  • Disability certificate / UDID, as applicable.
  • Aadhaar or another accepted identity proof.
  • Address proof.
  • Income certificate or other accepted income-related document, where required.
  • Passport-size photograph.
  • Any other documents requested by the implementing agency.

📝 How to Apply

  1. Find an authorised ADIP implementing agency.
  2. Complete the required application process.
  3. Submit the required documents.
  4. Attend assessment if required.
  5. Receive the eligible assistive device through the authorised process.

⚠️ Important Information

The type of device, amount of assistance and eligibility depend on the applicable ADIP guidelines and assessment.

Requirements and procedures may change. Always verify the latest information before applying.
