Annapurna Scheme

Scheme Overview

The Annapurna Scheme provides free food grains to eligible senior citizens who are not receiving benefits under the Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS). The scheme aims to ensure food security for elderly citizens.

Benefits

Eligibility

  • Applicant must be 65 years of age or above.
  • Must be an Indian citizen.
  • Should belong to a Below Poverty Line (BPL) family.
  • Should not be receiving benefits under the Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS).

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Age Proof
  • BPL Ration Card
  • Address Proof
  • Passport Size Photograph
  • Bank Passbook (if required)

How to Apply

  1. Visit your Gram Panchayat, Municipal Office, or Social Welfare Office.
  2. Collect and fill out the Annapurna Scheme application form.
  3. Attach all the required documents.
  4. Submit the application for verification.
  5. After approval, food grains will be provided through the Public Distribution System (PDS).
