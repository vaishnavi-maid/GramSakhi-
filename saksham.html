Scheme Name

AICTE Saksham Scholarship

Category

Students with Disabilities

Description

AICTE Saksham Scholarship provides financial assistance to specially-abled students pursuing technical education in AICTE-approved institutions.

Eligibility

  • Indian Citizen
  • Disability of 40% or more
  • Admission in an AICTE-approved institution
  • Family income as per scheme guidelines

Benefits

  • Financial assistance for education
  • Support for tuition and study expenses
  • Encourages higher technical education

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Disability Certificate
  • Income Certificate
  • Admission Letter
  • Bank Passbook
  • Passport-size Photo

How to Apply

  1. Visit the AICTE Scholarship Portal.
  2. Register your account.
  3. Fill the application form.
  4. Upload required documents.
  5. Submit the application.
