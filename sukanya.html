Scheme Name

Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)

Category

Girl Child

Description

Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana is a government savings scheme that helps parents save money for their daughter's education and marriage.

Eligibility

  • Girl child below 10 years of age
  • Indian citizen
  • Only one account per girl child

Benefits

  • High interest rate
  • Tax benefits under Income Tax Act
  • Secure government-backed savings
  • Supports education and marriage expenses

Required Documents

  • Birth Certificate of the girl child
  • Aadhaar Card of parent/guardian
  • PAN Card
  • Address Proof
  • Passport-size Photograph

How to Apply

  1. Visit the nearest Post Office or authorized bank.
  2. Fill out the Sukanya Samriddhi Account form.
  3. Submit the required documents.
  4. Deposit the initial amount.
  5. Receive the passbook.
