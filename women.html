🤝 Self Help Groups (SHGs)

A Self Help Group is a small community-based group where members save money together, support each other and work towards financial independence.


Benefits:

  • Regular savings
  • Access to credit
  • Skill development
  • Income generation
  • Women empowerment
  • Community development

🏛️ Government Support

Women can explore government programmes related to self-employment, entrepreneurship, skill development, financial inclusion and SHG activities.


👉 For detailed scheme information, visit the Government Schemes section of GramSakhi.

  • 🎨 Handicrafts
  • 📱 Digital Marketing

💼 Women Entrepreneurship

GramSakhi encourages rural women to turn their skills into sustainable businesses.


Steps:

  1. Identify your skill
  2. Choose a business idea
  3. Learn required skills
  4. Prepare a simple business plan
  5. Explore financial support
  6. Start and promote your business

💰 Financial Support

SHG members may explore bank-linked credit, microfinance and government-supported programmes. Always verify eligibility and current terms before applying.

🛍️ Promote Local Women Businesses

GramSakhi can help connect villagers with women-owned local businesses such as tailoring, food products, handicrafts, farming products and home-based services.
