🌱 Why Soil Testing is Important

Soil testing helps farmers understand the nutrient status and important properties of their soil. It can help in making better decisions about fertilizers, soil management and crop selection.

🔬 What is Soil Testing?

Soil testing is the process of examining a soil sample in a laboratory to determine its physical and chemical properties and nutrient status.


The test results can help farmers and agricultural experts recommend suitable nutrient management for a particular field.

🧪 What is Tested in Soil?

⚗️ Soil pH

Shows whether soil is acidic, neutral or alkaline. Soil pH affects nutrient availability to plants.

💧 Electrical Conductivity

EC gives an indication of soluble salts present in the soil.

🌿 Nitrogen (N)

Nitrogen is important for plant growth, leaf development and chlorophyll formation.

🌱 Phosphorus (P)

Phosphorus supports root development, energy transfer and reproductive growth.

🌾 Potassium (K)

Potassium supports plant strength, water regulation and crop quality.

🔬 Micronutrients

Depending on the soil test, nutrients such as zinc, iron, boron, manganese and copper may also be assessed.

🪣 How to Collect a Soil Sample

1

Select the Area

Divide the field into reasonably uniform areas based on soil type, crop history and appearance.

2

Avoid Unusual Spots

Avoid sampling from places such as manure heaps, compost piles, bunds, waterlogged areas or fertilizer storage locations.

3

Collect Soil

Take samples from the recommended soil depth using a clean tool and collect several representative spots from the field.

4

Mix the Samples

Mix the collected soil thoroughly in a clean container and prepare a representative sample according to local soil-testing instructions.

5

Remove Unwanted Material

Remove stones, roots, leaves and other unwanted material from the sample.

6

Label the Sample

Write useful details such as farmer name, field identification, village, crop and sampling date.

7

Submit the Sample

Submit the prepared sample to an appropriate soil-testing laboratory or agriculture facility.

8

Keep the Report

Keep the soil-test report safely and use it when planning nutrient management.

📊 Understanding Common Soil Test Parameters

ParameterWhat It Tells You
pHIndicates soil acidity or alkalinity and influences nutrient availability.
ECIndicates the level of soluble salts in soil.
Organic CarbonProvides an indication of soil organic matter status and soil health.
NitrogenIndicates available nitrogen status.
PhosphorusIndicates available phosphorus status.
PotassiumIndicates available potassium status.
MicronutrientsMay identify deficiencies or adequate levels of nutrients such as zinc, iron and boron.

🌾 Benefits of Soil Testing

💰 Reduce Unnecessary Cost

Helps avoid unnecessary fertilizer application when combined with proper recommendations.

🌱 Better Crop Growth

Balanced nutrient management can support healthy crop growth.

🌍 Protect Soil

Avoiding excessive fertilizer use can contribute to better long-term soil management.

📈 Improve Planning

Soil-test information can help farmers and agricultural experts plan nutrient management for crops.

🏢 Where Can Farmers Test Soil?

🔬 Soil Testing Laboratory

Farmers can submit samples to suitable government or approved soil-testing laboratories.

🌾 Agriculture Department

Contact the local agriculture office for information about available soil-testing facilities and procedures.

🏫 Krishi Vigyan Kendra

KVKs can provide agricultural guidance and may assist farmers in accessing soil-testing services.

👨‍🌾 Agricultural Expert

Farmers can discuss their soil-test report with a qualified agriculture professional before making major nutrient decisions.

⚠️ Important:

Do not apply a fixed quantity of fertilizer only from general information on the internet.

The correct recommendation can vary according to soil-test results, crop, variety, field conditions, previous crop and local agricultural recommendations.

For exact fertilizer recommendations, consult the Agriculture Department or a qualified agricultural expert.
