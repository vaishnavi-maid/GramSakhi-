Scheme Name

PM Vidya Lakshmi Scheme

Category

Students

Description

A government portal that helps students apply for education loans through banks.

Eligibility

  • Indian Citizen
  • Admission in recognized college
  • Eligible for education loan

Benefits

  • Easy education loan application
  • Single online portal
  • Loan tracking facility

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Admission Letter
  • Income Certificate
  • Passport Size Photo
  • Bank Details

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official website.
  2. Register an account.
  3. Fill the application form.
  4. Upload documents.
  5. Submit the application.
