📋 About the Programme

The Government of India implements programmes for prevention and control of non-communicable diseases (NCDs) through the public healthcare system.

🎯 Main Purpose

To promote prevention, early detection, screening, treatment and management of important non-communicable diseases.

🩺 What Are Non-Communicable Diseases?

Non-communicable diseases are health conditions that are generally not transmitted directly from one person to another and often require long-term prevention and management.

🔎 Important NCDs

❤️ Hypertension

High blood pressure can increase the risk of heart disease, stroke and other health complications.

🩸 Diabetes

Diabetes affects blood glucose levels and may require regular monitoring and long-term management.

🎗️ Common Cancers

Public health programmes support awareness and screening for selected common cancers.

❤️ Cardiovascular Diseases

Prevention and management of cardiovascular risk factors are important parts of NCD control.

🎯 Objectives

  • Promote healthy lifestyles and disease prevention.
  • Encourage early identification of NCDs.
  • Provide screening through appropriate healthcare facilities.
  • Support diagnosis and treatment through the public healthcare system.
  • Encourage regular follow-up and disease management.

🏥 Major Services

🔍 Screening

Screening services can help identify selected NCDs and risk factors at healthcare facilities.

🩺 Diagnosis

People with suspected conditions can be referred for further assessment and diagnosis.

💊 Treatment

Appropriate treatment and medicines may be provided through designated government health facilities.

📅 Follow-up

Long-term follow-up and management may be provided according to the patient's condition and healthcare plan.

🌱 NCD Prevention Tips

  • Eat a balanced and nutritious diet.
  • Stay physically active.
  • Avoid tobacco use.
  • Avoid harmful use of alcohol.
  • Maintain a healthy body weight.
  • Get recommended health screenings.
  • Follow medical advice for diagnosed conditions.

📝 How to Access NCD Services

  • Visit your nearest government health centre.
  • Ask about NCD screening services.
  • Get your blood pressure and blood glucose checked when recommended.
  • Follow the referral process if further examination is required.
  • Continue regular follow-up for diagnosed conditions.

⚠️ Important Information

Screening is intended for early identification and does not by itself confirm a diagnosis.

If you have symptoms or concerns about your health, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Availability of specific services may vary between healthcare facilities.

🌐 Official Information

For official information about national non-communicable disease programmes, visit the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare.
