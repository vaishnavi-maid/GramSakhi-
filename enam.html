Scheme Name

National Agriculture Market (e-NAM)

Category

Online Agricultural Marketplace

Description

e-NAM is an online trading platform that connects farmers, traders, and buyers across India. It helps farmers sell their agricultural produce at competitive prices through transparent online bidding.

Eligibility

  • Indian farmers
  • Licensed traders
  • Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs)
  • Registered agricultural buyers

Benefits

  • Online crop selling
  • Better market prices
  • Transparent bidding
  • Digital payment facility
  • Access to markets across India

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Bank Passbook
  • Mobile Number
  • Farmer Registration Details
  • Passport-size Photograph

How to Apply

  1. Visit the e-NAM portal.
  2. Create a farmer account.
  3. Complete KYC and registration.
  4. Add your crop details.
  5. Sell your produce through online bidding.
