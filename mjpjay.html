📋 About the Scheme

The Mahatma Jyotirao Phule Jan Arogya Yojana (MJPJAY) is a health protection scheme of the Government of Maharashtra. It aims to provide cashless healthcare services to eligible beneficiaries through a network of empanelled hospitals.

🏥 Main Purpose

To help eligible beneficiaries access healthcare services and reduce the financial burden of covered medical treatment.

🎯 Objectives

  • Provide financial protection against eligible medical expenses.
  • Improve access to quality healthcare.
  • Provide cashless treatment for covered procedures at empanelled hospitals.
  • Support economically vulnerable beneficiaries in accessing healthcare.

✅ Key Benefits

  • Cashless treatment for covered procedures.
  • Treatment through empanelled public and private hospitals.
  • Coverage for specified medical and surgical procedures according to the scheme package.
  • Reduced financial burden for eligible beneficiaries.
  • Access to a network of participating healthcare facilities in Maharashtra.

👨‍👩‍👧 Eligibility

Eligibility depends on the beneficiary categories and identification criteria specified by the Government of Maharashtra for the current scheme.

📋 Beneficiary Identification

Beneficiaries may need to provide an accepted government identity document and other required records.

🏠 Maharashtra Residents

The scheme is implemented in Maharashtra for eligible beneficiary groups according to applicable government rules.

📄 Documents Generally Required

The exact documents depend on the beneficiary category and verification process.

  • Aadhaar Card or accepted identity document
  • Eligible beneficiary/family identification
  • Ration Card or applicable document, where required
  • Mobile number
  • Other documents requested by the hospital or authority

📝 How to Get Treatment

  • Check your eligibility for MJPJAY.
  • Find an empanelled hospital.
  • Carry the required identification documents.
  • Complete beneficiary verification at the hospital.
  • Receive treatment under the applicable scheme package.

⚠️ Important Information

The list of covered procedures, eligibility categories, hospitals, documents and other scheme conditions may be updated by the Government of Maharashtra.

Always check the latest information from the official MJPJAY portal before applying or receiving treatment.

Visit the official MJPJAY website for the latest information about eligibility, hospitals, procedures and beneficiary services.
