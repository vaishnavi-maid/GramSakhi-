Scheme Name

PM Internship Scheme

Category

Students

Description

The PM Internship Scheme provides internship opportunities in leading companies to help students gain practical work experience.

Eligibility

  • Indian Citizen
  • Age 21–24 years (as per scheme guidelines)
  • Eligible students or fresh graduates

Benefits

  • Industry experience
  • Monthly financial support
  • Skill development
  • Better employment opportunities

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Educational Certificates
  • Passport Size Photo
  • Bank Account
  • Mobile Number

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official portal.
  2. Register yourself.
  3. Complete your profile.
  4. Select an internship.
  5. Submit the application.
