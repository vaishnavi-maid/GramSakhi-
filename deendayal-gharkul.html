📋 About the Scheme

Pandit Deendayal Upadhyay Gharkul Yojana is a housing initiative implemented in Maharashtra for eligible households from specified disadvantaged communities.

The scheme aims to support eligible families in obtaining a permanent house and improving their living conditions.

🎯 Main Objective

To provide housing assistance to eligible families who do not have adequate permanent housing.

👨‍👩‍👧 Eligibility

  • Applicant should be a resident of Maharashtra, subject to applicable rules.
  • Applicant must belong to a community or beneficiary group covered by the scheme.
  • Applicant should satisfy the applicable income and housing conditions.
  • Priority and beneficiary selection are carried out according to government guidelines.
Important: Exact eligibility requirements can vary according to the applicable government guidelines and beneficiary category.

💰 Key Benefits

  • Financial assistance towards construction of a permanent house.
  • Support for eligible families to improve their housing conditions.
  • Assistance is provided according to applicable government norms.
  • Housing support may be coordinated with other applicable government programmes.

🏠 Scheme Features

🏡 Permanent Housing

The scheme focuses on helping eligible families obtain permanent housing.

🌿 Maharashtra

The scheme is implemented within Maharashtra through concerned authorities.

👨‍👩‍👧 Eligible Families

Assistance is provided to beneficiaries who satisfy the prescribed conditions.

🏗️ Construction Support

Eligible beneficiaries receive support for house construction according to applicable rules.

📄 Documents / Information

  • Aadhaar card or approved identity proof.
  • Maharashtra residence proof, where applicable.
  • Income certificate, where required.
  • Caste or category certificate, where required.
  • Bank account details.
  • Land or house-related documents, where applicable.
  • Any additional documents requested by the concerned authority.

📝 How to Apply

  • Contact the concerned Gram Panchayat, local authority or government department.
  • Ask about the current application process.
  • Obtain the prescribed application form or use the official online process, if available.
  • Submit the required documents.
  • Follow the application verification process.

⚠️ Important Information

Eligibility, beneficiary selection, assistance amount and application procedure may change according to Maharashtra Government guidelines.

Applicants should verify the latest information with the concerned government authority before applying.
